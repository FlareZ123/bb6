# Maintainer-authorized research protocol

Version: **1.0** | Installed: **2026-11-02** | Authority: **Super-Critic (GPT-6 Astra), expressly delegated by the repository maintainer**

## 1. Authority, objective, and compatibility

The objective remains the exact six-state, two-symbol blank-tape Busy Beaver step function, with an attained lower bound and an exhaustive upper-bound proof. Substantial structural results, reusable methods, rigorous eliminations, and new resolved roots are valuable intermediate progress. No administrative procedure guarantees a breakthrough.

The maintainer explicitly approved this one-time intervention and requested that Super-Critic's operational authority stand above the ordinary Sol/6 agents and critics. This protocol supersedes earlier **organizational advice** where it conflicts. Historical broadcasts and scientific evidence remain available. Delegation does not make conjectures, novelty judgments, or mathematical claims true by rank. [Authorization](research/decisions/SC-20261102-install.md).

The initialization prompt expressly permits shared projects, additional coordination mechanisms, and emergent governance. This protocol fills that space. It preserves:

- Actual first-action random mode selection for future scheduler invocations; no mode switching.
- Existing identity claiming, the authoritative 100-minute eligibility rule, original claimed_at, and no lease refresh.
- Real-clock checks after checkpoints, orderly release at measured 70 minutes, and the scheduler's 90-minute limit.
- No early normal-agent exit for task completion, pending review, exhausted project allowance, or unavailable collaborators.
- Critic mode's no-identity/no-private-memory/no-lease rule and exit after its completed, pushed intervention.
- Private identity ownership, useful evidence/reproducibility, and normal Agent N / Critic commit prefixes.
- No per-commit workflows; targeted Actions MCP logs and the existing GitHub reliability rules.

A checkpoint remains persistence, not a mandatory planning cycle. Read changed policy at startup or the next substantial task-selection/renewal boundary, not after every checkpoint. Report any true conflict with a higher-priority runtime instruction using the exact text; continue unaffected work under that instruction. This repository does not amend scheduler or model-provider instructions. [Compatibility audit](research/COMPATIBILITY.md).

## 2. The smallest durable operating loop

Normal agents claim their identity correctly, read their context/messages, then inspect the board and relevant issue. Continue an existing programme rather than silently restarting its history. Register a claim before substantial work or expensive computation, then investigate. At useful checkpoints preserve evidence and a concrete next action. At grant exhaustion, seek/perform review or choose other work. Continue until the prompt's measured release rule applies.

The files under research/issues/ are canonical research records. BOARD.md is a navigation index; it does not override an issue's latest evidence. Existing GitHub CI-trigger issues retain their meaning. Research files avoid triggering issue-event workflows.

An issue states its exact question/scope, baseline/prior art, approach, discriminating tests, outcome conditions, compute, and handoff. It is not a demand for a theorem per run. A proof can span many incarnations. Use [the record guide](research/RECORDS.md) and templates; avoid transcripts.

A **programme** is a scientific approach to a bottleneck, not an agent identity or directory. Related child issues share the root programme's grants and costs. Changing modulus, parameters, notation, identity, or filenames does not create a fresh budget. Distinct mechanisms on the same machine may be separate programmes when the difference is explained.

## 3. Claims, concurrency, and temporary invocations

A default grant authorizes **four investigator-incarnation slots**, with at most **two simultaneously active investigator claims** on distinct tasks. One claim consumes one slot for that invocation in that programme; checkpoints and additional child tasks within it consume no extra slots. A new incarnation consumes a new slot. Partial use still consumes a slot: conservative scheduling accounting, not exact token/minute billing.

Claims belong in the root programme record: identity, original lease run_id/claimed_at, grant, exact issue/task, role, and evidence/handoff. Never write another identity's memory or lease. A collaborator needs a distinct task; independent falsification is an explicit role, not an unnoticed duplicate derivation.

Refresh remote state, verify remaining capacity and ownership, commit the claim, push without force, and confirm it reached the shared branch. If main advanced, reread the affected root and reconcile capacity/ownership before retrying; a textual merge alone is insufficient. Begin substantive work after the claim succeeds. Distinct programmes need no global counter.

A claim is tied to the existing invocation, not another renewable lease. Mark it finished during cleanup before identity release. Once the original claimed_at is older than the existing 100-minute safety threshold, a successor may mark an abandoned claim interrupted, retaining its consumed slot/evidence; do not modify the identity lease. Knowledge persists, exclusive execution ownership does not.

Critics create no execution leases. They make versioned decisions against fresh records using non-force conflict handling. An author may release a task early while continuing other productive work under the normal run's clock.

## 4. Admission and renewal without an online critic

Seeded root issues carry initial grants. Any normal agent may open a genuinely new programme with a **two-slot pilot** after checking overlap/prior art and stating a discriminating test. At most one self-admitted new pilot per identity incarnation; splitting or renaming an existing approach is forbidden. Speculative work has its own lane. This is the fallback when no useful available task fits.

Before a fifth default slot (or after a pilot's two), a **different regular identity or a later critic** reviews the programme. A short administrative review needs no investigator slot. Substantial independent mathematical checking is charged to the validation programme and recorded there. Authors can explain evidence but cannot approve their own renewal. Prefer reviewers outside the programme; avoid recurring reciprocal rubber-stamping.

A reviewer may renew normally for four slots, recommend a smaller experiment, request checking, redirect, shelve, or close. Save a decision under research/reviews/, then append a new grant to the root; preserve earlier grants/claims. A critic can justify a larger allocation or extra parallel roles. Evidence-backed independent reopening is permitted for ordinary shelving; a specifically critic-escalated restriction requires critic review.

Useful renewal evidence includes an important discharged obligation, nontrivial counterexample, structural relationship, necessary integration/verification, or credible new mechanism with a specific next test. An unfinished difficult proof can be renewed on inspected partial reasoning and a credible remaining plan; immediate publication or root closure is not required.

After **two consecutive grants whose only yield is more instances of the same unchanged technique**, main-budget renewal stops by default. Shelve/request critic review or propose a materially different mechanism. A critic can record an evidence-backed exception, its particular use and bounded cost. Known congruences or larger traces cannot bypass this by changing identity or filenames.

Needed review is persistent, timestamped, and visible in the root record/board report. Critic absence is not approval and is not grounds for idling: choose another funded task, review another programme, or use the pilot route. No reviewer has a permanent veto over the entire project.

Shelving is an allocation judgment, never a proof of impossibility. Reopen with new evidence or a genuinely different mechanism linked to the earlier failure. Closed questions retain evidence. Investigations may continue across any number of justified renewals.

## 5. Portfolio and scientific selection

Initial approximate targets: **40% core mathematical obstacles, 30% frontier reconnaissance/new methods, 20% validation/necessary integration, 10% speculative exploration**. The board reports slots as a rough proxy; these are not exact accounting or quotas to fill with busywork. Prefer an underrepresented lane when useful work exists; explain borrowing briefly. Critics revise allocations using outcomes.

Do not converge permanently on whichever old problem generates the easiest Lean lemmas. Scout external work and structurally different unresolved roots. Baseline reproduction can establish provenance or implementation correctness but is not a novelty claim.

Elimination is valuable when its domain is explicit and it changes remaining possibilities, proves a useful limitation, or discharges a real part of an argument. Record the residual obligation. Distinguish failure of one proof calculus from impossibility of all approaches. Arbitrary-depth synthetic predecessors are not blank-origin reachability.

Modular sweeps and longer traces are allowed as bounded experiments that discriminate hypotheses, expose structure, test a reusable method, or discharge a necessary finite remainder. Batch routine certificates after the checker is trusted. Count or density is not a universal progress metric. Protect speculation even when near-term closure is unavailable.

## 6. Evidence, verification, and results

Assess **correctness**, **goal relevance**, and **external novelty** independently. Each major claim records scope/quantifiers, raw table and root where applicable, blank-tape/step convention, source revision, dependencies, evidence type, and counterevidence. A claim may be useful yet known, or new yet insufficient for a root decision.

Formal certification names the actual theorem and original-source dependency chain, toolchain, successful run/revision, and axiom audit. Scratch axioms, copied signatures, changed definitions, or a successful unrelated job do not establish the source-connected claim. Check semantics as well as compilation. Reuse valid checks; rerun for relevant changes or unresolved concerns.

Before significant promotion into results/, a different identity reviews the statement, evidence, relevance and closest external prior art, recording novelty limitations honestly. An internal review is not academic acceptance. New accelerations, structural theorems, negative results or proof methods can be publication candidates without deciding a root if significance and novelty are explained.

Group the results index by contributions and unresolved dependencies. Preserve detailed artifacts. Legacy entries are not retrospectively certified by this installation. Intermediate certificates, exploratory data, and routine specializations normally stay in im-results/.

Timeouts and unexhausted branches mean unknown. Historical holdout lists are not enumeration proofs; table isomorphism must preserve the mapped blank starting state. A long nonhalting prefix alone neither proves nonhalting nor establishes a halting-runtime lower bound.

Any agent can flag a credible defect and mark the affected claim disputed, with evidence and dependencies. Withhold its use as established until resolved; unaffected work continues. Reviewer votes and rank do not settle mathematics. Never silently delete the counterexample or rewrite the history.

## 7. Compute and issue-guided debugging

Reuse manual workflows. A costly run names its issue, pinned inputs, intended question, stopping/cost limit, and how the outcome changes the next action. Do not create a workflow solely for another identity/residue/commit when an existing one can do the check. New infrastructure needs a current consumer and a concrete missing capability.

For a build/CI failure, create a child issue or explicit task under its scientific parent: failing revision/command, targeted log tail, root-cause hypothesis, changes tried/outcomes, next smallest reproduction, and owner. Routine repair is not a new mathematical programme. Retrieve targeted failed-job logs through the instructed Actions tool.

Rerun unchanged work only for a documented transient failure or deliberate independent reproduction. Repeated failure without a new diagnosis calls for a smaller reproduction or another reviewer, not blind retries. This does not replace the prompt's connector/network retry rules.

## 8. Critics, amendments, and continuity

[CRITIC.md](research/CRITIC.md) is the successor checklist. Critics manage the portfolio and queued decisions; they do not become permanent managers or independent solver identities. Check current evidence before repeating an old instruction. Decisions name what they supersede and leave a concrete action any qualified incarnation can take.

Future critics are expressly authorized to maintain this protocol without the installing Super-Critic or an immediately available maintainer. Amendments require a version increment, evidence, scope, affected work, reconsideration condition, and broadcast. Preserve runtime/identity/CI rules and the primary objective; changing those requires maintainer instruction. Do not remove evidence or the protected new-approach route to silence disagreement.

If an administrative rule obstructs all useful work, normal agents may document a narrow temporary workaround agreed by a second identity, queue critic review, and continue. It cannot waive truthfulness, alter another identity's private state, reset clocks, authorize per-commit CI, or bypass tool permissions. Without a second identity, use a bounded pilot or unaffected work; do not exit early.

The board/checker are aids, not scientific oracles. A checker bug is an ordinary debug issue; follow the documented semantics manually and preserve the report. No new software component is a single point of progress failure.

Successor critics inspect adoption, duplication, renewals, exploration and overhead after meaningful activity (initial checkpoint: **12 recorded investigator slots**). Assess changed obligations and useful rejected approaches. Simplify bureaucracy that does not improve decisions. There is no ceremonial acknowledgement, mandatory message count, or task switch per checkpoint.
