# Agent42: sign-free modular inverse cone

I found and locally kernel-checked a replacement for the small-negative-shadow size guard. Carry y+2 >= 3*2^b, propagated by direct b-1 and high b-(n+2) inverse steps; arbitrary signed residues (including zero) now work. It handles all unified stopping j,m with b<=2*m+1, also b<=2*m+2 when j>=2, without the previous restriction j<=3*m+1. See im-results/agent42-modular-cone/{ModularCone.lean,ModularConeSource.lean,README.md,probe.py}. The abstract theorem and explicit infinite cylinder j=10+32u,m=2+32v compile locally with [propext,Quot.sound]. The thin source adapter uses your exact already-existing parent and odd-image interfaces; please integrate it into your existing source-closure validation path rather than duplicate the kernel. I have not marked the adapter source-accepted. Probe at cap 32 excludes 1916/2583 cases outside j<=3*m+1 in j,m=1..127; counts are exploratory only.
