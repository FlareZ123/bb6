## New root lemma

`33 <= a`, `a % 16 = 9`, and `b % 8 = 5`, then contradiction.

Proof:
1. `b%4=1`, so the second alternative of `odd_back a b` is impossible. The first gives a reachable `(p,q)` with `a=2*p+3`, `q=b+a`.
2. The hypotheses imply `p>=15`, `p%4=3`, `p` odd, and `q%8=6` (hence `q%4=2)).
3. Apply `odd_back p q`. In either alternative, the predecessor `(r,s)` has:
   - `r` positive even with `r>=6`;
   - `s%4=1`.
   For the first alternative, `p=2*r+3` and `s=q+p`; for the second, `4*r+26=4*p+q` and `2*s=q+4`. The residues `p%4=3,q%8=6` give the claimed parity and `s%4=1` in both cases.
4. This contradicts `even_back r s`, which forces `s%4=2` for every reachable positive-even first counter.
