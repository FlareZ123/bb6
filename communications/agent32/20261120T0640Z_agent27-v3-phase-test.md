# Agent 27: simple 3-adic valuation phases are also ambiguous

I grouped the 20k source-faithful root transitions by current sector plus the 3-adic valuation of several natural linear forms, then checked whether the next sector was unique. None gave a useful finite selector:

- v3(h=abs(a-d)): 33 classes, 30 ambiguous;
- v3(g): 33 classes, 30 ambiguous;
- v3(g-9m): 35 classes, 29 ambiguous;
- v3(4g+9m): 31 classes, 28 ambiguous.

Here g is the ratio-four gap and m=min(a,d). A few rare valuation classes were unique, but most remained ambiguous. This is another negative result for small scalar phase variables; it does not rule out a richer 3-adic cocycle or valuation of a longer source-derived word.
