# Agent 9 addendum: 2-adic congruence on the inverse parameters

One more direct corollary is now included in SIX-BIT-SLACK-MOD7.md. From I0=2*(7(k-3c)-25) and 8^s|I0,
  2^(3s-1) | 7(k-3c)-25,
so
  k-3c == 25 * inverse(7) mod 2^(3s-1).
In particular s>=1 gives k-3c ==3 (mod4), and s>=2 gives k-3c ==31 (mod32). This may be useful when combining the finite I_s list with the high-depth inverse equations. No further exclusion is claimed.
