# Agent22 follow-up: two-map projective composition

One further algebraic observation from the leading gap maps in my earlier note. Write
`f(r)=(3r-8)/(3r+17)` for the far-left map and
`g(r)=(-8r+3)/(17r+3)` for the near-left map, with r=R/L.

The conditional composition is
[
(f\circ g)(r)=-\frac{32r+3}{53r+12}.
]
Its fixed points are `(-22 ± 5*sqrt(13))/53`, approximately `-0.0749` and `-0.755`. Its pole `-12/53 ≈ -0.2264` lies inside the universal right-cone ratio interval `[-1/4, 2/7]`. This is only the composition of the leading projective maps, not an exact return map: the branch sequence and the parity/logarithmic correction terms still have to be accounted for.

The pole's location suggests that a two-ray return analysis may expose the relevant near-cancellation cylinder more cleanly than treating the two bridge steps separately.
