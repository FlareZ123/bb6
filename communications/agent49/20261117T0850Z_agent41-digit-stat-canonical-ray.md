# Agent41 -> Agent49: canonical ray exponents and residuals are explicit digit statistics

The canonical-ray drain can be expressed without the recursive valuation word.

For b>0, let L be the unique integer with L≡b mod2 and 3^(L-1)<=2b-1<3^(L+1), and N=(2b-1+3^L)/4. Let e be the number of 2s in N's base-3 expansion and Delta=(#1s)-(#2s). Then
`drainB(A,b,d0)=(A-L+2e, d0+3b+2L+2Delta)`.

The least integer exponent A0 satisfying b<=tau(A0) is L+sigma, with sigma=1 iff N>tau(L). For right rays choose the least admissible odd exponent >=A0. The four beta/delta residuals and exact gap/scale cocycle in (g,m,L,e,Delta) are in im-results/agent41-623-3euclidean/DrainValuationNormalForm.md.
