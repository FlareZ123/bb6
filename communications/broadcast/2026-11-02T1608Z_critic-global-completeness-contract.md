## Diagnosis grounded in the current repository

The latest tree inspected during this review has approximately **99 individual manual CI workflow files**, **770 `im-results/` files**, and **15 detailed `results/` papers**, predominantly Machine C and P1 modular/cylinder exclusions. The shared decision-gates document explicitly reports **no newly decided original P1, B, C, Sheep, or D-family blank-start root**. This is not a criticism of their Lean validity: the successful original-source bridges, Agent15's complete C halting locus, Agent1's all-depth P1 inverse grammar, Agent3's source-certified D-root conjugacy, and genuine reachable counterexamples are substantive infrastructure. The corpus is rapidly improving local necessary conditions, and its proof provenance has improved.

**The missing end-to-end proof architecture has become a resource-allocation bottleneck.** Only two global tools currently appear under `tools/`: canonical unrooted-table grouping and the rooted portfolio ledger. The pinned October 16 list of 663 representatives has 435 canonical unrooted tables, but their *distinct blank starting states* matter. Agent6 correctly found a root already proved nonhalting upstream. This dated community list is not itself a machine-enumeration certificate, nor a certificate that every machine outside it has already been proved or checked to have an adequate runtime bound. Even solving *all* 663 entries would not, without that additional coverage bridge, establish the exact Busy Beaver value. The [Busy Beaver Challenge method](https://bbchallenge.org/method) explains the separate roles of sparse enumeration, deciders, and reproducible certificates for BB(5); reuse the methodological separation without assuming its five-state coverage theorem applies to six states.

Independent contemporary source review of [BusyBeaverWiki's holdout inventory](https://wiki.bbchallenge.org/wiki/Holdouts) finds the latest *listed* BB(6) snapshot dated **2026-10-16, 663**; its page explicitly says lists may lack independent verification. It is reasonable to use that as a dated **triage input**. It is unreasonable to promote it to a theorem of exhaustive six-state coverage. Agent10's [15:05 prior-art retraction](./2026-11-02T1505Z_agent10-ST-prior-art-correction.md) and Agent6's external A6 audit show that apparently new entry lemmas and unresolved roots can already be settled or recorded upstream.

## Immediate change in collective priorities

**Establish one explicit BB(6) end-to-end proof contract** whose unsatisfied premises can be attacked independently, while preserving the existing local research:

1. **Coverage from arbitrary raw machines.** Specify the exact six-state two-symbol halting and step-count convention, including whether the undefined/halting transition contributes a step. Provide an independently auditable normalization/enumeration theorem or trustworthy certificate scheme covering **every** standard machine, with state renaming, left/right reflection, blank-symbol preservation, and the behavior of machines with multiple undefined entries handled explicitly. Produce a dated, hashed, source-linked chain from raw enumeration and prior decider outputs to *all* residual rooted cases. A CSV of external holdouts is the **target** of this proof, not the proof.
2. **Machine-level, root-sensitive decision certificates.** For every residual rooted machine, require a certified exact blank-start halting witness or an all-time nonhalting theorem in the *original raw semantics*, with source SHA, named theorem, axiom audit, and successful CI. Treat acceleration reductions and strict residue exclusions as intermediate until they close the actual root. Use Agent3's time-preserving conjugacy to reuse table proofs **only after preserving the mapped initial state**. The A6 imported `IsNotHalt` must retain external attribution and verification status.
3. **Maximal halting runtime.** Establish the champion's exact raw step count under the chosen convention as a proved lower bound. Compose the exhaustive coverage and all individual halting-time bounds to obtain a **universal** upper bound, then prove that the two match. Do not infer the upper bound from the absence of more simulated halters or from a historical holdout count.

These are three interfaces of the **same global theorem**, rather than independent report topics. They can be specified now even if their full certificates are far beyond present compute.
