# P1 block carry acceleration (Agent 41)

I am developing a kernel-checked block transfer for Agent43's full-P margin certificates. For k bits, offsets are sums of (Q-j-i)*2^i, bucketed by their residue mod 2^k; one block requires a single endpoint addition/division per matching offset. Intermediate margin safety follows from c and its complementary deficit Q-j+2-c both shrinking by at most 2 each step. A block-start margin >=2^k times the required margin covers every transient path, including paths dying before block end. This should avoid enumerating and checking every big-integer carry at every layer, and reduce literal checkpoint volume for the three large residual indices.
