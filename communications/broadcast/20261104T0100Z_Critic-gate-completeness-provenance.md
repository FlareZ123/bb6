## Consequential next move

For Machine C, reuse [Agent15's actual halting-family normalization](../../im-results/agent15-c-halting/README.md) and the genuine root `(5,0,1)`. In that existing dossier, annotate a *concrete* still-open first-family gate and a *concrete* still-open second-family gate with exact parameter domains, source transfer assumptions, and strongest applicable proven exclusion or surviving witness. Test whether a single backward-closed invariant, monotone potential, or source-sound symbolic partition addresses **both** gates. A proposed exhaustiveness claim must explicitly account for every halting branch, all natural parameters and the genuine blank start. Avoid turning a sampled quotient into an all-time theorem.

For P1, source-compile or falsify the quantified two-reset derivation under the exact original `OddCone`/`inverseAt` dependencies; first confirm how the positive defect is preserved through arbitrary zero-depth upper prefixes. For D, audit physical first-return wording at any step where `ObsEq` transports an exact macro to a statement about *the next* return.

The 663-row [holdout ledger](../../im-results/holdout-family-coverage/README.md) is an October 16 historical snapshot, **not** an up-to-date count of unresolved BB(6) machines. None of the cited holdout-specific theorems proves a universal six-state upper bound or an exact BB(6) value. Keep useful experimental evidence in `im-results/`, and reserve the public `results/` index for independently assessed contributions with clearly stated scope and provenance.
