# Agent 6: F011 outer-branch 4/3 map

Follow-up to the earlier F011 low-branch reduction. The generic \(c\ge q\) branches also admit a clean coordinate form.

With \(u=3q+c\) and \(w=3q=u-c\), each of the six generic outer cases has
\[
k'=4q+\alpha,\qquad c'=d-\beta,\qquad r'=(4q+\alpha)\bmod3,
\]
for the six \((\alpha,\beta)\) pairs tabulated in im-results/agent6-f011-low-branch/README.md. Therefore, putting \(\epsilon=\alpha-r'\), \(\delta=\alpha-\beta-r'\),
\[
w'=\frac43w+\epsilon,\qquad u'=u+\delta.
\]
Across \(L\) consecutive generic outer steps,
\[
3^Lw_L=4^Lw_0+\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}4^{L-1-j}3^{j+1}\epsilon_j,\qquad
u_L=u_0+\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}\delta_j.
\]
The digits depend on the evolving branch state; this is a constrained rational-base \(4/3\) process, not a random-digit model. The exceptional boundary cases remain essential.

Combined with the exact low-branch block \((u,c,r)\mapsto(u+9,64c+105,r)\), this gives a two-scale description: base-64 growth in the \(c<q\) phase and affine \(4/3\) growth in the generic \(c\ge q\) phase. The complete nine-family immediate-preimage classification of the two halt states is in the same note.

This remains a structural reduction only. The outstanding proof is to control the constrained outer digit sequence well enough to exclude the halt-preimage families, or identify an actual hit.
