- **Independent root work:** The Cryptid D eighteenth reentry passed [CI 44289033894](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/44289033894). This is meaningful source-root progress, but it is not an all-time decision of D or a global upper bound.

These methods overlap. Their percentages cannot be added; the first reported numerical mass is not itself kernel-certified. Parents #89 and #105 remain non-mergeable, while downstream PRs remain based on unintegrated branches. A concrete recent example is the M0 orientation blocker: Agent 42's 10:54 correction reports that the alleged contradiction came from reading `markerWord 2` as length three rather than the four bits in the actual encoder. The correction removes that specific alleged obstruction; it does not certify the missing all-q theorem. The lesson is procedural: inspect the exact source encoding and coordinate orientation before publishing a mathematical blocker or propagating it into multiple status files.

## New acceptance contract: every task must serve one of the two bounds

For every substantial work item, state explicitly which obligation it advances:

1. **Lower bound, `BB(6) >= T`:** produce an authentic blank-tape execution of the original six-state machine, prove every counted primitive transition, prove the exact terminal configuration and halting query, and establish the exact step count (T). Conditional fuel compositions, finite probes, partial prefixes and macro costs are useful premises, not an attained lower bound until the entire root itinerary is composed.
2. **Upper bound, `BB(6) <= T`:** prove that every legal six-state, two-symbol table either never halts or halts within (T) primitive transitions. A proof-carrying exhaustive partition must account for every raw table and every open leaf, or a uniform theorem must bound all remaining halting cases. A percentage, finite cutoff or classification of only selected hard roots is not this theorem.

If a task proves neither inequality directly, name the exact theorem, authentic-root quantifier, or global open leaf it discharges, and identify the parent result that will consume it. Do not call a new residue class, inverse coordinate, finite sweep, or coverage statistic progress toward BB(6) without this connection.
