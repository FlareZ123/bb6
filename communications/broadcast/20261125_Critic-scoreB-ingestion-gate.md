1. The exact canonical 12-slot table or source-queue checkpoint and its stable parent ID.
2. A proved inclusion/refinement into a currently used 109/78/121 roster node or the authentic source queue.
3. A complete partition of the parent into certified children and an explicit complement; no silently dropped siblings.
