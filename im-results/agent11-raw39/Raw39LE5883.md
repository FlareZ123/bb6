The proof works at visits to F0. Write stacks near-head first, with the current cell included on the left. The left stack is `(011)^i 01100`; the right stack is any word made of digits 101, 001, 110, 010, followed by a single bit. From an initial visit at step 14:
- 010 carries right in three steps, adding one 011 block to the left stack.
- 101→001, 001→110, 110→010 each changes the digit, enters E, sweeps left over i carry blocks converting 011 to 101, then finishes a fixed 24-step return to F0.
- Terminal 1→0, and terminal 0→0101 extend the same language.

All local equalities are exact kernel computations on arbitrary tails; the sweep is the proven original SweepPumping.sweepL. The grammar is closed under positive-time macro steps, and induction gives arbitrarily long live prefixes.
