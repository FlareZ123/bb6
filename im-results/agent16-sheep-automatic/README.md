# Obstruction to permutation-automaton invariants of the Sheep section map

## Claim and scope

For the exact Sheep raw section map F (seed 5), no language recognized by a finite deterministic permutation automaton on canonical least-significant-bit-first binary words can contain 5, be forward closed under every genuine F edge on integers >=5, and omit every terminal power of two >=4.

This is a mathematical argument, not a Lean theorem. It uses the previously established mathematical [mixed residue connectivity](../agent22-sheep-mixed-hub/MIXED-COMPLETENESS.md) and [logarithmic phase corridors](../agent17-sheep-scale-mixing/README.md). It handles arbitrary finite permutation transition groups, not just congruence predicates. It does not rule out general deterministic automata with noninvertible transitions, and does not decide the authentic orbit.

## 1. Stable congruence kernel for a finite word group

Let G be the finite group generated by the two letter actions A (zero) and B (one); multiply in reading order. For a finite binary word w let g(w) be its action, v(w) its LSB-first numeric value and n(w) its length. Empty words and padded words are allowed in this auxiliary algebra. For any odd m and positive L, the map

    w -> (g(w), (v(w) mod m, 2^n(w) mod m), n(w) mod L)

is a monoid homomorphism to a finite group, using (r,t)(s,u)=(r+t*s,t*u) in the affine component. Its image J is a subgroup (inverses are positive powers). Define K(m,L)={g: (g,(0,1),0) belongs to J}; it is normal in G since the projection J -> G is onto. If m divides m' and L divides L', then K(m',L') is a subgroup of K(m,L).

Choose m0,L0 minimizing the cardinality of K over all pairs. Enlarge L0 to a multiple of ord(A) and ord_m0(2), with order 1 for m0=1. The kernel K cannot shrink under any such refinement, and consequently remains identical at every odd multiple m of m0 and every multiple L of L0. For two words with equal value modulo m0 and equal length modulo L0, their group images lie in the same coset of K: their affine and length components agree, so their quotient in J has identity in both components.

## 2. Every progression-phase cell realizes its whole group coset

Fix a positive modulus M divisible by m0, a residue r modulo M, and a nonempty open interval J0 of log2(x) modulo L0 lying strictly inside one unit interval (so binary length modulo L0 is fixed). In the progression x=r mod M with phase in J0 and x above any prescribed bound, canonical binary words realize the entire unique K coset for that residue and length.

Proof: factor M=2^s*m with m odd and choose L a multiple of L0 and ord_m(2). Fix a low block l of s bits representing r modulo 2^s (empty when s=0). Fix a high canonical block h ending in one, long enough to put every number with that high prefix and desired total length phase inside J0. This is possible because the binary leading-prefix intervals refine the mantissa interval. Write the word l v h, with a middle block v of length n chosen sufficiently large in the desired congruence class modulo L. The condition modulo m is

    value(l)+2^s*value(v)+2^(s+n)*value(h)=r mod m.

Because 2 is invertible mod m and 2^n is fixed in the length class, choose a solution for value(v) and pad to n bits. For any k in K=K(m,L), there is a word z with g(z)=k, value(z)=0 mod m, length(z)=0 mod L, and 2^length(z)=1 mod m. Inserting z between v and h preserves the low block, high prefix, residue, and length class. The transformations g(l v) k g(h) run over the whole K coset by normality. Taking the original middle length sufficiently large ensures the lower bound for all insertions. This proves the claim, including for any progression refinement used below.

## 3. A filled even cell forced by the actual seed

Write H for the accepted group elements (those mapping the initial state into an accepting state). Put p=ord(A). For every positive a divisible by 2p, x=5*2^a is accepted, because its word is 0^a followed by the word for 5. Its genuine generic raw successor is y=5*2^a+a+2, which is even. For sufficiently large a, delta=a+2 < 2^a. Its canonical word is w(delta), then a-length(w(delta)) zeros, then w(5). Thus

    g(w(y))=g(w(delta)) A^(-length(w(delta))) g(w(5)).

Choose a fixed residue of delta modulo lcm(2p,m0) compatible with delta=2 mod 2p and a fixed binary length modulo L0. By section 2, g(w(delta)) realizes a full K coset for arbitrarily large delta in this progression. The power of A is fixed because p divides L0. Normality gives an entire K coset of accepted even y's. All even words in the corresponding residue-length cell modulo 2*m0 and L0 have transformations in that same coset; in particular this cell is filled.

Call a cell filled if its entire K coset is in H. Cells are the even residues modulo m'=2*m0 paired with unit intervals of phase modulo L0 (boundary powers are assigned by binary length). The coset depends only on the residue and length, so filled means every number in the cell is accepted, including boundary points.

## 4. Propagation of filled cells

For any genuine generic even return x0 -> y0 with valuations (a,h), the published equations give arbitrarily large lifts

    x(d)=x0+2^(a+h+1)*d,
    y(d)=y0+3^h*(2^(a+1)+1)*d

for even d>=0. Restrict d to multiples of m'. Then residues are unchanged and y(d)/x(d) tends to a positive constant lambda. Suppose a cell of the source residue is filled. Choose an interior source phase c for which c+log2(lambda) is not a cell boundary. For sufficiently large members of the target progression in a suitably small open interval around that target phase, the source is in the original filled cell. Closure along genuine raw edges forces acceptance of every such target. Section 2 then gives a full target coset, so a target cell is filled.

The mixed-residue theorem implies connectivity on even residues modulo m'; indeed its dyadic exponent is 1, so it is a complete directed graph. Thus every even residue has at least one filled cell.

## 5. Filling all phases

Use the two previously established genuine even corridors

    x_i=4^(k-i)*5^i*v-2,
    z_i=8^(k-i)*9^i*v-12,   0<=i<=k,

for even v>=14. Restrict v to multiples of m', so each corridor stays at a fixed residue. Their limiting endpoint log-ratio is respectively k*log2(5/4) and k*log2(9/8). The ratio of those two logarithms is irrational by unique prime factorization. At least one log-ratio divided by L0 is therefore irrational. Fix that corridor and its residue, which already has a filled cell. Positive multiples of its rotation are dense modulo L0. For any desired target cell choose k mapping an interior source phase to the target interior. The endpoint progression has an open subinterval in which, for sufficiently large v, all corresponding starts are accepted. Closure along the corridor forces acceptance of all those endpoints. The realization lemma forces the entire target coset. All cells at this residue are filled.

Finally lift a genuine generic edge from this residue to every other even residue. Now every sufficiently large source in the lifted progression is accepted regardless of phase. In each target cell apply the realization lemma to the target progression. All cells become filled. In particular all sufficiently large powers of two are accepted, contradicting the required terminal exclusion.

## Status

This is a structural obstruction for reversible binary word recognizers, not a statement that arbitrary regular languages cannot be invariants. In particular, a general DFA need not admit a positive zero-prefix pumping cycle at the initial state, and its transformations form a monoid rather than a group. The proof does not extend to those cases without additional argument. The all-modulus connectivity and real rotation argument are mathematical dependencies, not an end-to-end Lean verification. No new BB(6) bound follows.
