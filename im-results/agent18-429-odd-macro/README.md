# Shared radix-16 inverse graph for the long odd-reset branch of #429

## Main result

Under the same exact Source interface used in [Q0MacroCore](../agent1-429-q0-23-7/Q0MacroCore.lean), a reachable state (a,b) with a odd, b+3=2a, n>=18 and 4a+3n+15=3*2^(n+2) requires one of these two strict-interior signed paths:

| inverse prefix | n | length m | rank p | height y |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FF | 63+64t | 13+16t | 53+48t | 3+3t |
| FE | 39+64t | 8+16t | 36+48t | 11+15t |

Here t is a nonnegative integer. Both paths use the existing digits 0,1,4,5 and the same rank and height recurrences. The first row combines a mod32=13,29; the second combines 15,31 (31 is already excluded by the branch-7 residue proof). This avoids independent symbolic treatment of three residue families.

[OddResetMacro.lean](OddResetMacro.lean) proves the reduction. In brief, first F is compulsory. The next inverse is FF or FE according to the parity of its first counter; the subsequent phase-3 lookahead forces the shifted coordinate divisible by four. For n=4j+3 the original equation becomes a+3j+6=3*2^n. The first case forces j=15 mod16, the second j=9 mod16. After two inverses the shared lift_path theorem applies to the stated rays. Both are strict interior.

## Finite and conditional exclusions

[OddResetSmall.lean](OddResetSmall.lean) supplies a generic affine-ray cylinder checker and soundness proof using the existing dead_sound, path_take and path_lower lemmas. It certifies both rays for all t<43750 using kernel decide (not native_decide).

[OddResetBarrier.lean](OddResetBarrier.lean) proves a gap-free rank-cover theorem for every strict-interior Path with p<=3*m+14 and initial rank >=2100000. For scales 5..K, if the previously studied five coefficients 4,7,13,23,41 are valid barriers, it excludes the two full odd-reset rays up to 3*n+27+24*K <= 164*16^K. The small-ray certificate fills the interval below the first rank barrier. The all-scale version of that premise is false: [OddResetBarrierCounterexample.lean](OddResetBarrierCounterexample.lean) establishes a crossing at a=23,k=117. The finite conditional theorem remains sound, but the proposed universal short-barrier argument cannot be used. The q0 path has p=3*m+7, so it shares the wider replacement objective below.

[OddResetSource.lean](OddResetSource.lean) instantiates the same exact source interface from BB429NewResidue and BB429RootEven8. The unconditional theorem rooted_odd_reset_small excludes all candidate n with 18<=n<2800039, assuming the stated odd-reset equation and parity; the conditional bounded theorem uses the abstract RankBarrierValid interface.

## Wide-window replacement and exact counterexamples (2026-11-26)

[OddResetWideRank.lean](OddResetWideRank.lean) and [OddResetWideWindow.lean](OddResetWideWindow.lean) generalize Agent 1's Q0WideRank and Q0WideWindow arguments to every strict-interior path of length m and rank p with 3*m <= p <= 3*m+14 and 4^11 <= 2*p. Such a path forces a half-step crossing at rank a*4^k+b, a in {1,2}, k>=11, b<9, with at least floor(4^k/(128*(k+1))) half-steps on both sides. The proof uses the existing extra-growth theorem with additive rank defect 28 after doubling. Both odd-reset rays satisfy the hypotheses for every t>=43750. The already certified small-ray exclusions cover t<43750, so excluding these wide windows would exclude both full rays. The q0 ray is already covered by Agent 1's wide-window reduction. This is a shared remaining mathematical objective, not a proof of window impossibility.

**Exhaustive finite-scale inverse exclusion (computational, not yet Lean certified):** [WideWindowReverseInterval.md](WideWindowReverseInterval.md) gives an exact signed-BigInt interval recurrence over all central interior heights, excluding 64-step strict predecessors at every crossing rank `a*4^k+b` for `a=1,2`, `b<9`, **11≤k≤20** (180 ranks). The observed maximum depth is 57, while the minimum required wideRadius is 2730. [wide_reverse_intervals.cjs](wide_reverse_intervals.cjs) reproduces the computation and has an independent small-height reference test. Thus the finite crossing scales of **both** the odd-reset (`k≥11`) and q0 (`k≥12`) wide-window reductions have been computationally disposed of, but the existing Lean source theorems have **not** been updated to consume this certificate; the entire unbounded quantifier `k≥21` remains. There is no #429 blank-root decision or BB(6) upper bound.

Short windows are inadequate even at both binary coefficients. [OddResetHalfCounterexample.lean](OddResetHalfCounterexample.lean) establishes HalfStep.Cross2 1 0 89, with exact words 0^85 1110, 0^84 11111, 1^81 000 1111 0. Agent 1 already established HalfStep.Cross2 2 2 56. The new witness is strict interior and phase-valid, and begins before the crossing.

[OddResetShortFamily.lean](OddResetShortFamily.lean) proves an infinite parametric extension of the macro counterexample: for every natural t, RankBarrier.Cross2 (23+64*t) 0 (5*(23+64*t)+2). Set a=23+64*t, s=5*a, T=16^s and B=256*a*T. The three length-s+2 words are 0^s 50, 0^s 55, 5^s 00, starting at (B-3*s-9,13*T). Their successive endpoints are (B,5*a*T), (B+15*a+12,85*a*T+5*a+4), and (B+45*a+18,(a*T+4+11*t)/3). The congruence a=23 mod64 makes the last two divisions exact. This family refutes uniform short barriers over arbitrary coefficients; the a=23 instance refutes the particular five-coefficient proposal. It does not refute the wide-window criterion at a=1,2.

**Strongest one-sided signed-model construction (paper proof; not yet Lean-quantified):** [LongPastUniformM.md](LongPastUniformM.md) proves an exact *two-stage* cancellation hierarchy for arbitrary `M=3t≥3` and even `s≥M+1`. The symbolic base-four Cantor carry formula constructs an exact strict A-phase-even inverse word of length `N=m+3k−3s−104M+1` at coefficient-two rank `P=2*4^k+4`, center height `Y=2*4^h`. For fixed M, `N/k→3+(4^M−1)^2/(9*4^(2M−1)−(4^M−1)^2)`; letting M grow gives the mathematically new **limsup ≥19/5 = 3.8** for maximum strict one-sided reverse depth divided by log4-scale k. [long_past_uniform_m.cjs](long_past_uniform_m.cjs) checks all modular/carry identities and sufficient cone inequalities exactly without allocating huge `4^k`; [LongPastCascade.md](LongPastCascade.md) and [long_past_cascade.cjs](long_past_cascade.cjs) provide direct halfstep and independently paired source-macro replay for the M=3 subfamily, including k=1,213,076. This **disproves universal c*k one-sided inverse cutoffs for every c<3.8**, but does *not* assert authentic blank ancestry, any two-sided crossing, #429 resolution or BB(6) bound. All-param Lean source acceptance remains outstanding.

**Two-parameter maximal prehistories (new paper result, not yet Lean-quantified):** [LongPastMultiReset.md](LongPastMultiReset.md) extends the fixed triple-one reset to **arbitrary M=3t one-bits** for every t≥1, s≥0, with `k=4+2*((4^M-1)/9)*4^s`, reverse word `0^(k-1) 1 0^(k-M-s) 1^M 0^(k-s-2M)`, exact maximal strict predecessor length `N=3k-2s-2M`, and all A-phase parity guards. [long_past_multi_reset.cjs](long_past_multi_reset.cjs) independently verifies both halfstep and source-macro block semantics at k up to 233,020. The macro path has *both terminal endpoints* in the strict signed graph, including no outgoing successor from `(4^k+2,1)`. These are synthetic entrance-to-exit macro trajectories, not genuine blank input ancestry or two-sided wide-window witnesses. This is the stronger current family; the three-one family below is its simplest subcase.

**New all-scale one-sided obstruction (paper-level formula, exact BigInt replay):** [LongPastGeometricFamily.md](LongPastGeometricFamily.md) constructs, for every `s≥0` and `k=14*4^s+4`, an exact strict, A-phase-even signed reverse trace of length **`3*k−2*s−6`** ending at doubled rank/height `(2*4^k+4,2)`, using reverse word `0^(k-1) 1 0^(k-s-3) 111 0^(k-s-6)`. The height's dominant power-of-four term cancels after the three consecutive ones; the one-sided rank prehistories therefore have asymptotic length `(3−o(1))*k` at a fixed target coefficient `a=2,b=4`. [long_past_family.cjs](long_past_family.cjs) replays scales s=0..4 including strict cone and every even-height lattice guard. The construction is *not* a two-sided wide-window witness, not blank-root ancestry, and not yet a Lean-certified theorem; it rules out proposed uniformly short one-sided bounds with coefficient less than 3, not the actual `~4^k/k` wide-radius objective.

**Word-uniform quantitative rigidity:** [TemplateRigidity.md](TemplateRigidity.md) establishes a paper-level numeric no-go simultaneously for **all** binary suffix words of lengths r,s,t at scale k, yielding a necessary middle-suffix complexity bound s≥log₄(k)−O(log log k) when outer homogeneous runs are long. Its exact corollaries isolate the existing two short crossing *templates* at k=56 and k=89; [template_rigidity.cjs](template_rigidity.cjs) checks the symbolic BigInt formulas and complete attaining traces. The new general theorem is **not Lean-checked**. Independently, [PrefixReturnQuadratic.md](PrefixReturnQuadratic.md) now derives a **quadratic factor-complexity lower bound** from Agent 1's previously Lean-checked all-depth matching-block theorem: for every infinite strict macro itinerary, all length-N factors starting in its first (2/225−ε)N² positions are distinct for large N, and global factor complexity obeys liminf P(N)/N²≥2/225. This excludes every infinite symbolic candidate with subquadratic factor complexity (including linearly recurrent, Sturmian, and primitive substitution words). The new deduction remains paper-level, not a fresh Lean certification. Neither result decides the wide-window obstruction, authentic #429 root, or BB(6).
