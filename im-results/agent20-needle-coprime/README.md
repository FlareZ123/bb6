## Original blank-root quadratic growth and square-root reachability decision

The strongest new result is a source-connected, universal **two-step square-root gain**. For any two successive successful high-level transitions in the *original six-state Space Needle*, writing the first shifted counter as `b=d+2` and the counter after both transitions as `B`, the source-exact theorem proves `b ≤ (B-b)^2`. Its proof uses binary-valuation divisibility, with no dynamical conjecture. It implies for **every finite successful genuine blank-root prefix** of length `n`, even when that orbit later halts,

```text
(floor(n/4)+1)^2+1 ≤ d_n+2.
```

The resulting complete fixed-target reachability bound is `n < sqrtFuel(d) := 4*(floor(sqrt(d+1))+1)`. A single-pass executable Boolean decision `sqrtScan(d)`, proved equivalent to `ReachN d`, checks at most `O(sqrt(d))` genuine high-level transitions. It is a stronger *actual blank-root* decision method than the original linear and this dossier's earlier `O(d/log d)` scans. It remains exponential in the binary length of `d` and does not eliminate the unbounded existential over terminal targets. [Full two-step proof, precise theorem scope, and reusable checker](QuadraticRootGrowth.md). Exact source theorem modules: [NeedleTwoStep.lean](NeedleTwoStep.lean), [NeedleQuadratic.lean](NeedleQuadratic.lean), and [NeedleQuadraticSource.lean](NeedleQuadraticSource.lean). **Original-source certified** in [successful CI 42732043121](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/42732043121), importing the unchanged pinned original Cryptids machine; final theorem audits report only standard Lean axioms, no `sorryAx`.

The independently certified **one-step** bound `b'-b≥floor(log2 b)+2` gives a simpler finite-prefix restriction `floor(n/2)*(floor(log2 n)+1)≤d_n+2` for `n≥2`; its derived exact `optimizedScan` decides `ReachN d` after `O(d/log d)` high-level checks.
