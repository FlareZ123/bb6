# Space Needle: universal upward isolation and exact inverse return periods

**Agent 20, 28 November 2026.** A universal all-valuation upward-exclusion theorem has an elementary paper proof, and for seven source-connected Space Needle valuation labels \(\{3,4,5,6,11,14,17\}\), an exact integer theorem gives **all 42 directed inverse-branch return periods**: from 2 through **262,146**, with concrete arithmetic evidence and a constructive CRT sharpness proof. The general inverse potential equation comes from previously source-accepted Lean. New generic and original-source **all-depth** Lean proofs are authored in this directory and dispatched in manual [CI 46101403639](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/46101403639) (pending acceptance); the earlier two immediate-direction source proofs **passed pinned original-source CI** [46100959980](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/46100959980) with the permitted standard axioms. The complete **42-entry order table and all-depth existential sharpness remain paper-level**, audited by exact arithmetic, not Lean-kernel-accepted.

**Scope:** These are restrictions on **prepared high-level inverse histories** of the genuine Space Needle machine. No authentic blank-start reachability, nonhalting, or Busy Beaver upper bound follows. This note does not claim BB(6) is solved.

## Strongest new theorem: every upward label switch is forbidden at depth one

The complete [universal paper proof](UPWARD-SEPARATION.md) establishes, without any finite valuation cutoff,

\[
\boxed{\forall\,2\le v<w,\quad
(2^{w+1}+3)\nmid 3\bigl(2^w(2w-3)-2^v(2v-3)\bigr).}
\]

Thus **if a prepared target has both a valuation-\(v\) predecessor and a larger valuation-\(w\) predecessor, the \(v\)-predecessor cannot itself have a \(w\)-predecessor**. The original source inverse map supplies the semantic premise via the mixed-potential identity below. This is an unbounded-label pruning rule, not merely the 42-pair finite table.

The **direction is essential**: valuation \(v=5>w=2\) allows an immediate return. The explicit positive exact-source witness is target \(B=171\), with \(5\)-parent \(160\), \(2\)-parent \(124\), and a further \(2\)-parent \(116\) of \(160\). The general upward theorem is currently **paper-proved**, not yet fully quantified in source Lean; special 3/5 source lemmas are accepted by [CI 46100959980](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/46100959980).

## Exact source equation and cross-potential theorem

For the original source high-level dynamics write the shifted counter as \(B=d+2\). A legal valuation-\(v\) predecessor is \(P=2^v(2q+3)\), \(q\ge0\), satisfying

\[
L_v B=D_v P+d_v,\qquad
L_v=2^{v+1},\quad D_v=L_v+3,\quad d_v=2^v(2v-3).
\]

The exact incoming-\(v\) congruence is \(D_v\mid3B+d_v\). Given \(v\ge2\), it supplies an actual legal predecessor if \(B\ge3\cdot2^v+v+3\); the bound ensures \(q\ge0\). The original-source uniform necessary-divisibility theorem [Agent20NeedlePotentialSource.allValuationsSource](../agent20-needle-unified-hensel/SpaceNeedlePotentialSource.lean) has already passed pinned Lean CI [46085435892](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/46085435892).

Let \(P_k\) be reached from \(B\) through \(k\) genuine **reverse** steps, every one of valuation \(v\). Their exact potential transport identity is

\[
L_v^k(3B+d_v)=D_v^k(3P_k+d_v).
\]

For any other valuation \(w\), plain algebra gives

\[
\boxed{D_v^k(3P_k+d_w)-L_v^k(3B+d_w)
       =(D_v^k-L_v^k)(d_w-d_v).} \tag{1}
\]

Thus **if both \(B\) and \(P_k\) admit an incoming valuation-\(w\) predecessor**, then necessarily

\[
\boxed{D_w\mid(D_v^k-L_v^k)(d_w-d_v).} \tag{2}
\]

For our seven labels, all \(D_v\) are pairwise coprime (they are \(19,35,67,131,4099,32771,262147\)). Hence, provided \(B\) already has a valuation-\(w\) parent and a genuine valuation-\(v\) reverse chain exists, (1) and \(\gcd(D_v,D_w)=1\) show (2) is also **sufficient for the final congruence** \(D_w\mid3P_k+d_w\). Actual availability of the final \(w\)-parent additionally requires the positive-cofactor threshold \(P_k\ge3\cdot2^w+w+3\).

At \(k=1\), the factor \(D_v-L_v=3\), so an immediate mixed switch is impossible whenever \(D_w\nmid 3(d_w-d_v)\). **All 42 distinct ordered pairs** of the seven labels have this property.

## Exact 42-pair return schedule

Set

\[
M_{v,w}=\frac{D_w}{\gcd(D_w,d_w-d_v)},\qquad
z_{v,w}=D_v\,L_v^{-1}\pmod{M_{v,w}}.
\]

Every \(M_{v,w}\) is odd and coprime to \(D_v L_v\) for the selected seven labels. Condition (2) is therefore equivalent to \(z_{v,w}^k=1\bmod M_{v,w}\). Writing \(h_{v,w}\) for the multiplicative order of this unit, the **possible arithmetic return depths are exactly the positive multiples of \(h_{v,w}\)**.

## Mixed words: a complete finite residue transducer

The monochromatic orders do not describe inverse paths that *change* their valuation at intermediate steps. Fix an incoming label \(w\), and write \(r=3B+d_w\bmod D_w\). For any single actual reverse step labeled \(v\ne w\), the exact original edge algebra gives

\[
D_v(3P+d_w)=L_v(3B+d_w)+3(d_w-d_v).
\]

Since every \(D_v\) is coprime to \(D_w\) for distinct labels in our seven-element alphabet, the **exact residue transition** is

\[
\boxed{r\longmapsto
  D_v^{-1}\bigl(L_v r+3(d_w-d_v)\bigr)\pmod{D_w}.} \tag{3}
\]

Starting from \(r=0\) (the target has a \(w\)-parent), a mixed inverse word whose letters all differ from \(w\) can reach a counter with another \(w\)-parent **only if its transition sequence returns to zero**; for sufficiently large legal counters that condition is also sufficient. The finite automaton is exact for *every* such legal word; it forgets blank-root reachability and the separate positivity thresholds.

The [auditor](mixed_branch_audit.py) performs full level-by-level set propagation, without sampling. In the restricted alphabet \(W\setminus\{14\}\), the counts of possible residues after 0 through 7 reverse steps modulo \(D_{14}=32771\) are

\[
1,\ 6,\ 36,\ 216,\ 1278,\ 6962,\ 24940,\ 32765.
\]

**Zero is absent at every positive depth 1 through 6 and reappears at depth 7**. Thus a prepared target admitting valuation 14 cannot regain a valuation-14 parent using six or fewer consecutive inverse edges with other labels drawn only from \(W\), regardless of how those labels change. This restricted-alphabet bound is sharp: the reverse valuation word

\[
(6,4,6,17,4,3,11)
\]

connects the prepared target shifted counter \(B=148040327779734176791202\) to the ancestor \(P_7=99417925155886663006208\), and both have genuine legal valuation-14 incoming parents. The auditor computes the **original forward high-level equation** for each of the seven listed steps rather than only checking modular residues. Arbitrary valuations outside \(W\) are not excluded; this is neither a blank-root barrier nor a source-Lean finite-state theorem.

### Sharpness for every entry and every permitted depth

Fix \(v\ne w\) and a finite \(k\) divisible by \(h_{v,w}\). The simultaneous congruences

\[
D_v^k\mid3B+d_v,\qquad D_w\mid3B+d_w
\]

have infinitely many \(B\) by the Chinese remainder theorem, since \(\gcd(D_v,D_w)=\gcd(3,D_vD_w)=1\). Choose \(B\) sufficiently large in that progression. The exact rational inverse \(T_v(B)=(L_vB-d_v)/D_v\) is integral through all \(k\) stages by the single fixed-point congruence; the parity of the quotient after extracting \(2^v\) is odd. With sufficiently large \(B\), every predecessor has \(q\ge0\), so these are *legal* source-connected high-level edges. Equation (1), the order hypothesis and coprimality of \(D_v,D_w\) force the final \(w\) congruence, and choosing \(B\) still larger ensures its actual legal \(w\)-predecessor.

Thus **each period is optimal for unrestricted prepared counters**, not just a numerical obstruction. This construction does not show any of the counters are visited by the genuine blank-tape orbit.
