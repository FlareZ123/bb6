# Valuation-bounded exact-positive inverse cone fallback

For a target (x,y) with y >= 5, put o=(x+y)%2 and Q=x+y+o. Every ordinary high-bounce predecessor has a=Q/2^(n+1)-2-o and 2k=x+2y-a-7-3n, with n<=v2(Q), and the two exceptional high branches also have n<=v2(Q). Thus the search needs at most v2(Q)+1 levels rather than log2(Q)+1 and does not evaluate secNext on rejected candidates. Direct and small exceptional parents are included; no inverse claim for y<5 is needed because the checker rejects an unresolved small leaf.
