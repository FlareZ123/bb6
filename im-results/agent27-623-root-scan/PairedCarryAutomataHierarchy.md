# Paired-carry automata hierarchy for the #623 S-window proof

## Scope

This package starts from the committed 66-state `PostResetCarryPairClosure` automaton. It describes the paired-carry dynamics **after** a full-range reset has entered that closure. It does not replace the separate 263-state full-range reset recognizer, and none of these finite automata alone proves the all-time root invariant.

## Three exact finite representations

| Representation | States/elements | What it retains | Best use |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Transformation monoid | 475 | Exact action of every suffix on all 66 carry states | Exact suffix effect and composition |
| Image-subset automaton | 211 | Set of possible carry states after a suffix | Safe uncertainty propagation |
| Minimal synchronizing-language DFA | 41 | Only whether the suffix maps all 66 states to one | Cheap synchronization test |

### Transformation monoid

The full transformation monoid is generated by the nine digit-pair maps. BFS from identity closes after 475 transformations, with shortest representative length at most 6. Its rank distribution is 66 maps of rank 1, 288 of rank 2, 42 of rank 3, 63 of rank 4, 6 of rank 5, 6 of rank 7, 3 of rank 8, and the identity of rank 66. The identity has no incoming one-symbol transition, so every nonempty word induces a nonidentity map and therefore has image size at most 8.

See [PairCarryTransformationMonoid.md](PairCarryTransformationMonoid.md) and its JSON certificate.

### Image-subset automaton

Starting from the full 66-state set, the reachable image subsets number 211. Their sizes are 66 (initial only), 8 (3 subsets), 7 (6), 5 (6), 4 (21), 3 (30), 2 (78), and 1 (66). Every nonempty word reduces the possible state set to at most 8; every singleton target is reachable.

See [PairCarrySynchronizingSubsetDFA.md](PairCarrySynchronizingSubsetDFA.md).

### Minimal synchronizing-language DFA

Accepting exactly when the image subset is a singleton and minimizing yields a complete 41-state DFA with one accepting class. Its 820 unordered state pairs all have distinguishing suffixes of length at most 2: 40 pairs are separated by the empty word, 649 by one symbol, and 131 by two symbols. This is a compact minimality certificate.

See [PairCarrySyncDFAMinimality.md](PairCarrySyncDFAMinimality.md).

## Connection to the existing S-window trace

On the existing 9,968 transitions D33→D10001, the suffix after the first output of the 580-state buffer-relative reset DFA is accepted by the 41-state synchronizing DFA exactly when its 475-monoid transformation has rank 1: 9,884 accepted, 84 rejected, zero mismatches. The suffix rank distribution is 1:9,884; 2:61; 3:10; 4:5; 5:1; 7:1; 8:3; 66:3. The rank-66 cases have empty suffix; every nonempty suffix has rank at most 8.

These 84 nonsynchronizing suffixes occur at scattered source indices (last observed index 8539), so they are not merely a finite initial-prefix issue. For them, the 211-state image-subset automaton gives an exact uncertainty set of size at most 8 whenever the suffix is nonempty.

See [SWindowPostResetSynchronization.md](SWindowPostResetSynchronization.md).

## Recommended composition

1. Keep the 263-state full-range reset DFA for the first reset event.
2. At that event, recover the 66-state paired-carry seed from the output label and event symbol.
3. Feed the post-reset suffix into the 41-state DFA. If accepting, the end carry is independent of the seed; if rejecting, use the 211-state image-subset automaton to retain the at-most-eight possible states, or the 475-state monoid if exact action is needed.
4. Compose this post-reset summary with the source-faithful S-window low-boundary phase, low/high shifts, and high-extension digits.

The existing product using full phase, seed, end state, event buffer, and five-symbol tail is deterministic on the finite trace but has 9,968 distinct nodes and no repeats. It is not a closed all-time invariant. Tail lengths 3 and 4 leave two and one multi-successor collisions respectively; five symbols resolve all observed collisions.

## Proof status

All counts and automata here are exhaustive finite computations relative to the committed 66-state transition table. The JSON artifacts and generators are suitable inputs to Lean formalization, but the source closure, monoid, subset DFA, minimality witnesses, and S-window composition still need kernel-checked proofs. The next meaningful advance is a source-permitted closed product relation, not another root replay.
