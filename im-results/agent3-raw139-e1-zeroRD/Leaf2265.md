## Exact symbolic transition mechanism

Zeros extend either ray indefinitely; U and L below are arbitrary untouched tails unless the left ray is explicitly blank. Define P=01 and G=1100 (concatenation notation). Define S(k,W)=A(0^∞;P^k W0^∞) for finite W, canonically strip leading P pairs from W into k and discard trailing zeros. k is positive after the 13-move blank prefix S(3,empty).

### Full returns, all parameters

A rightward P-sweep costs 2k. An A1 reverse sweep of the left P-stack costs 2k and the blank exit costs 2. Thus

`S(k,1U) -> S(k+1,0U)` in 4k+2 moves.

For a suffix 00 1^m 0 U, first scan P^k and enter C through 00. The C1 sweep costs m. C0 followed by D1,E1,D0,F0 costs 5 and returns A three cells left of the C0 head. When m≥1, its left ray is 1^(m−1) P^k blank and its right is 1111U. When m=0, its left is 1 P^(k−1) blank and its right 0111U (k≥1).

The six-step A1,F1,D1,E1,D0,F0 identity pops four left ones and prepends G to the right after the scanned 1; iterate r times. If the fourth left bit is 0 it instead returns A scanning that 0 with right 01100R. If the second left bit is 0, D0 is defined and the D pair-drain applies. If the second left bit is 1 and the third 0, the first undefined query is E0 after the first three moves A1,F1,D1. This proves the following exhaustive section rules for k≥1, where r≥0:
