## Research implication

The observed growth of (
u_2(d-40a)) should not be interpreted by itself as convergence to the exact (m=48) feeder. The feeder family is dense along the 2-adic line, and the parameter of the nearest feeder can change with precision. A useful 2-adic argument would need to control the parameter (mu(a)) and its compatibility across root states, or exploit information beyond a finite residue cylinder. A finite-modulus exclusion of the entire family is unavailable at states lying in the indicated 2-adic neighbourhoods.
