# Source-certified exclusion of the known 108-call family at root section 31,777

**Status:** source-certified finite root segment plus the published characterization of the 108-call family. This excludes one specific empty-suffix section of the authentic root from that family; it is not an all-time nonhalting result.

## The known family

The BusyBeaverWiki analysis of #85/#490 gives a 108-macro halting itinerary using only the two non-reset branch types (the displayed representative is an E/B word). It reports 1,057,916,215,296 valid length-108 residue classes, generated by constrained permutations of the two allowed residue symbols; the third symbol resets progress. In the local A/B/E section notation, the displayed 108-call word contains only E/B, never A.

The representative word and residue base are recorded in `DeepMindStackReduction.md` and on the [wiki page](https://wiki.bbchallenge.org/wiki/1RB---_0RC0RE_1RD1RF_1LE0LB_1RC0LD_1RC1RA).

## Consequence

Every member of the known 108-call halting family is E/B-only. The source-certified near-shadow at section 31,777 takes an A branch at macro 15. Therefore **that particular authentic-root empty-suffix section cannot be any member of the known 1,057,916,215,296-word 108-call family**.

This is stronger than excluding only the displayed representative: the A branch excludes the entire E/B-only constrained-permutation family.

## What this does not prove

- It does not prove that the authentic root never halts.
- It does not exclude another A-containing terminal itinerary.
- It does not exclude a later empty-left reset from beginning a different E/B-only trial.
- It does not show that the *initial* blank-root section `A(9,"")` is outside every halting family; the result concerns the later empty-suffix section at index 31,777.
- The external family characterization is from the community wiki, not a theorem in the local Lean development.

The next useful target is an all-time invariant for every later reset/empty-suffix section, or a source-connected terminal witness with exact primitive-step count. The finite section-31,777 exclusion alone is not a BB(6) upper bound.
