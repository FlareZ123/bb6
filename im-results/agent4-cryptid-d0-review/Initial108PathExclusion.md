# The authentic root's 13-adic near-shadow of the known 108-call family

**Status:** useful finite arithmetic/simulator observation with a source-certified later continuation. The complete near-shadow-to-root composition is **not yet kernel-certified**; this is not an all-time nonhalting result.

## The known family

The BusyBeaverWiki analysis of #85/#490 gives a 108-macro halting itinerary using only the two non-reset branch types (the displayed representative is an E/B word). It reports 1,057,916,215,296 valid length-108 residue classes, generated by constrained permutations of the two allowed residue symbols; the third symbol resets progress. In the local A/B/E section notation, the displayed 108-call word contains only E/B, never A.

The exact representative word and residue base are recorded in `DeepMindStackReduction.md` and on the [wiki page](https://wiki.bbchallenge.org/wiki/1RB---_0RC0RE_1RD1RF_1LE0LB_1RC0LD_1RC1RA).

## The root's near-shadow

In the authentic-root arithmetic chain from `L(9,0,0)`, section index **31,777** has state `L(a,0,0)` and

```text

a - C ≡ 3^13 (mod 3^14)
```

The raw DRegular/A_step2 replay reports that the next thirteen macro branches from this section are exactly the first thirteen letters of the displayed halting word:

```text

```

After thirteen matching updates, the parameter difference is
`a_i - n_i = 8^i * 3^(13-i) * p`, where `p mod 3 = 1`. The raw replay then reports macro 14 = E and macro 15 = A, whereas the displayed representative continues B,E. Since every member of the known 108-call family is E/B-only, the computed 15-macro near-shadow is not any member of that family.

This is stronger than comparing only the single displayed representative **if the 15-macro itinerary from section 31,777 is correct**: the A at macro 15 excludes every E/B-only constrained permutation.

## Remaining scope

Even after the near-shadow composition is compiled, it would not prove that the authentic root never halts:

- another A-containing terminal itinerary may exist;
- a later empty-left reset may begin a new E/B-only trial;
- the wiki's 108-call family is not a classification of every possible halt.

The next useful proof task is to compile or replace `Root13Section.lean` so that the exact section-31,777 state and its thirteen-step arithmetic lift are connected to the original blank-root run. Only then should the E/B-family exclusion be promoted beyond computational evidence. A full verdict still needs an all-time invariant or a source-connected terminal witness with exact primitive step count.
