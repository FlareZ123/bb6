# Authentic-root exclusion from the known 108-call halting family

**Status:** exact source-prefix composition plus an external combinatorial characterization. This excludes one known family of halting itineraries from the *initial* empty-suffix trial; it is not an all-time nonhalting theorem.

## The known family

The BusyBeaverWiki analysis of #85/#490 gives a 108-macro halting itinerary using only the two non-reset branch types (the displayed representative is an E/B word). It reports 1,057,916,215,296 valid length-108 residue classes, generated by constrained permutations of the two allowed residue symbols; the third symbol is excluded because it resets progress. In the A/B/E section notation of the local D work, the third branch is A (the n mod 3 = 0 case), while E and B are the two branches used by the known 108-call family.

The exact representative word and residue base are recorded in `DeepMindStackReduction.md` and on the [wiki page](https://wiki.bbchallenge.org/wiki/1RB---_0RC0RE_1RD1RF_1LE0LB_1RC0LD_1RA).

## Consequence

Every one of the known 108-call halting itineraries uses only E/B and has no A. The authentic root's initial empty-suffix trial reaches an A branch at macro 15. Therefore **the initial root trial cannot be any member of the known 1,057,916,215,296-word 108-call halting family**.

This is stronger than comparing the root with the single displayed representative: it excludes the whole constrained-permutation family, provided the wiki's residue-family characterization is taken as stated.

## What this does not prove

- It does not prove that the authentic root never halts.
- It does not exclude another, unreported halting itinerary that uses A.
- It does not exclude a later empty-left reset from beginning a new 108-call trial.
- It does not turn the heuristic probability estimate into a theorem.

The correct next question is whether the root's later reset states ever enter *any* of the known halting cylinders, or whether a different A-containing terminal itinerary exists. A proof of all-time nonhalting still needs an invariant covering every possible halt gate; a proof of halting needs an exact source-connected terminal witness and primitive step count.
