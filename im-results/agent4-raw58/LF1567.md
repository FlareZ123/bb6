Let D(k) = D((100)^k 1011, empty). Each return D(k) → D(k+1) has positive finite cost. Consequently this leaf is nonhalting for every compatible completion.

The exact zipper macros implement a little-endian base-four counter. Digits 0,1,2,3 are 010,000,011,001 respectively. At B with left 01 and right 00 followed by a word of digits, a 10-step return toggles the right prefix to 01. The subsequent rightward E scanner carries over each maximum digit in three steps, building one 100 block on the left. When the next digit can be incremented, the reset sweep converts each carried block in nine steps and returns to the B boundary. When all digits are maximal, the scan terminates at D(length). D(k) enters a counter with k leading zero digits and digit 2, of total length k+1.

Well-founded recursion on R proves finite overflow of every finite digit word. Combined with the D(k) returns this proves unbounded live prefixes from the blank.
