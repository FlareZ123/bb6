# Finite-horizon quotient for secondary lifetimes

For a remaining horizon h, retain the subsequence of lifetimes at most h, plus the full count. A deleted entry has lifetime 1. A discarded lifetime x > h+1 is not deleted, and its successor (x−1)2^d > h for every preceding deletion count d. Thus removing it cannot affect any future death count before the horizon.
