# Second-layer R Hensel selector and unbounded odd-parent exponents

**Status: exact arithmetic derivation plus modular computation; not yet a Lean theorem.** This extends Agent 7's first-digit continuation test for the t=7 branch to a full 3-adic selector and then applies the dyadic band argument again.

## 1. Restrict to the continuing exact-Q lift

Use Agent 7's fixed-depth family
\[
n=887+13122r,\qquad j=3402.
\]
For t=7 and scale offset \(\sigma=14\), the Q selector gives
\(3^{10}\mid Q\) exactly when \(r\equiv4\pmod9\). Exact valuation
\(v_3(Q)=10\) occurs on \(r\equiv13,22\pmod{27}\). Agent 7's first-digit continuation test distinguishes these two lifts: the \(r\equiv13\pmod{27}\) lift has \(3\mid2h+5\), where h is the t=7 parent state; the \(r\equiv22\pmod{27}\) lift does not.

Parameterize the continuing lift by
\[
r=13+27u,\qquad n=171473+354294u
=171473+6\cdot3^{10}u.
\]
The exact next-layer numerator in Agent 7's note is
\[
R_{7,14}(n)=3^{10}(2h+5)=A2^{n-3}+Bn+C,
\]
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
A&=7\cdot2^{14}-3^{10}=55639,\\
B&=-(2^{10}+2^{3416}+2\cdot3^{10}),\\
C&=15\cdot2^9-3\cdot2^{3415}+9\cdot3^{10}.
\end{aligned}
\]
For even j, \(B\equiv1\pmod3\). The first-digit test gives
\(3^{11}\mid R_{7,14}(n(u))\) for every u, not just for the base point.

## 2. A full scaled Hensel selector

Define
\[
G(u)=R_{7,14}(171473+6\cdot3^{10}u)/3^{11}.
\]
The scaled-cylinder lemma in AFFINE-EXP-HENSEL.md applies with \(a=10\). For \(r\in\{0,1,2\}\),
\[
G(u+r3^k)\equiv G(u)+2Br3^k\pmod{3^{k+1}}.
\]
Since \(2B\) is a unit modulo three, G is a 3-adic isometry and has a unique zero modulo \(3^k\) for every k. Thus there is a compatible sequence \(\eta_k\) such that
\[
3^{11+k}\mid R_{7,14}(n(u))
\iff u\bmod3^k=\eta_k.
\]
This is the full nested selector; Agent 7's earlier test supplied only its first digit.

Exact modular evaluation of the closed formula gives
\[
R(n(0)),R(n(1)),R(n(2))
\equiv 3^{11},0,2\cdot3^{11}\pmod{3^{12}},
\]

## 3. The next dyadic band and unbounded second-layer exponents

For a further odd-parent exponent \(\ell\), the divisibility condition is
\[
3^{\ell+1}\mid2h+5
\iff 3^{\ell+11}\mid R_{7,14}(n(u)).
\]
The selector therefore supplies one residue class \(u\equiv\eta_\ell\pmod{3^\ell}\). Exact exponent \(\ell\) is obtained by removing its unique next-level lift, leaving two classes modulo \(3^{\ell+1}\).

Let \(\sigma'\) be a candidate scale offset and set
\[
\kappa=n+\ell-\sigma',\qquad
d_\ell=R_{7,14}(n)/3^{\ell+11},\qquad
x_\ell=2^\ell d_\ell.
\]
Since \(R_{7,14}(n)/2^n\to A/8\),
\[
\frac{x_\ell}{2^\kappa}
=\frac{2^{\sigma'}R_{7,14}(n)}{3^{\ell+11}2^n}
\longrightarrow
\frac{A\,2^{\sigma'-3}}{3^{\ell+11}}.
\]
Whenever this limit lies strictly between 6 and 8, the exact integer odd-parent band holds for all sufficiently large u in the selected residue classes. The quotient \(d_\ell\) is odd because \(R=3^{10}(2h+5)\) is odd. The parent is then given by
\[
p_\ell=2x_\ell-8\cdot2^\kappa-\kappa-5,
\]
so this is a genuine ResetParent predecessor of h.

The band-compatible exponent set
\[
S_A=\left\{\ell\ge0:\exists\sigma'\in\mathbb Z,\quad
6<\frac{A2^{\sigma'-3}}{3^{\ell+11}}<8\right\}
\]
has natural density \(\log_2(4/3)\). Indeed, the condition is equivalent to
\[
\frac{48\cdot3^{\ell+11}}A<2^{\sigma'}<
\frac{64\cdot3^{\ell+11}}A;
\]
the multiplicative interval has ratio \(4/3\), and the fractional parts generated by \((\ell+11)\log_2 3\) are equidistributed. Thus \(S_A\) is infinite. For every \(\ell\in S_A\), the unique Hensel cylinder contains infinitely many indices of exact valuation \(\ell+11\), and all sufficiently large such indices satisfy the dyadic band. **The second-layer branch therefore has unbounded further odd-parent exponents**, not merely a single additional parent.

Three concrete band-compatible instances are:

| 1 | 9 | \(3560896/531441\in(6,8)\) | \(u\equiv1\pmod3\) |
| 4 | 14 | \(113948672/14348907\in(6,8)\) | \(u\equiv31\pmod{81}\) |
| 6 | 17 | \(911589376/129140163\in(6,8)\) | \(u\equiv436\pmod{729}\) |

These cylinders are nested. In the original r parameter they are respectively
\(r\equiv40\pmod{81}\),
\(r\equiv850\pmod{2187}\), and
\(r\equiv11785\pmod{19683}\).
The first case was checked exactly at the least u=1: n=525767, \(v_3(R)=12\), the quotient is odd, and the t=1 band holds.

## 4. What this establishes and what it does not

This is a genuine extension of the Q/U feeder analysis: the exact t=7 lift \(r\equiv13\pmod{27}\) contains a full 3-adic continuation for the next odd-parent numerator, and the same irrational-rotation band argument gives unbounded further exponents at that second layer. It supplies reproducible selector residues and a general proof mechanism.

The result remains intermediate. The full derivation depends on Agent 7's source identity \(R=3^{10}(2h+5)\) and first-digit classification; the new scaled selector and second-layer band theorem have not yet been formalized in Lean or passed original-source CI. It does not establish that any of these branches reaches or excludes blank root 2, and it does not determine BB(6).
