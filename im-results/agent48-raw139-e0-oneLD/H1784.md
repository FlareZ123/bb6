## Two row modes

For s≥1,j≥0, let X(w,s,j,T)=A0(B(w),V^s Q Q^j T), with T either 1, blank, or 110 Q^i 1 (i≥0). Let Y(w,j,T')=A0(B(w),Q^j T'), with T' either 101, 11, or 100 Q^i 1 (i≥0).

**X return.** X has a positive run to Y(b^(s−1) a w,j+1,T'), with tail conversion 1→101, blank→11, 110 Q^i 1→100 Q^i 1. Use A-to-F, s−1 F loops, F-to-A, then j Q sweeps to obtain A0(P^j U^(s+1) 1 B(w),T). The turn and j P loops give D1(U^(s+1) 1 B(w),Q^j T'). Since s+1≥2, X-finish gives left 01 0 U^(s−1) 1 B(w)=01 (0011)^(s−1) 0101 e(w), using 0 U^r 1=(0011)^r 01. Right is Q^(j+1) T'. Cost 6s+7+14j+9+c, with c=8,6,4 respectively.

**Y with a zero digit.** Write w=a^u b z. Scan j Q, turn, then j P loops to D1(B(w),R0), where R0=Q^(j+1)1 (101), Q^j 101 Q^i1 (100 Q^i1), or Q^j1 (11). Apply 2u+1 of the 01 loops, then b-finish. Left is B(z), right V^(u+1) R0. Thus:
- 101 → X(z,u+1,j,1).
- 100 Q^i1 with i>0 → X(z,u+1,j,110 Q^(i−1)1), since 101 Q^i1=Q 110 Q^(i−1)1.
- 1001 → X(z,u+1,j,blank), since 1011 with blank continuation equals Q with blank continuation.
- 11 with j≥1 → X(z,u+1,j−1,1).
Cost 14j+4u+6+c, c=16,8,4 respectively. Composing with X performs little-endian binary increment a^u b z → b^u a z, preserving length.

**Y overflow in 101 mode.** For w=a^u, scan j Q, turn-101, j P loops, then 2u+1 of the 01 loops. This reaches D1(empty,(10)^(2u+1) Q^(j+1)1). Blank-left turn gives F0(11,V^(u+1)110 Q^j1), since (10)^(2u+1) Q=V^(u+1)110. Run u+1 F loops, then overflow-finish. Endpoint Y(b^(u+1),0,100 Q^j1), left 01(0011)^(u+1), right 100 Q^j1. Cost 14j+10u+36. No overflow law is required in 100 or 11 modes.

## Authentic infinite run

The 364-step blank entry (head displacement +4) reaches Y(aa,2,101). In Y(w,j,101), maintain n=length(w)≥2, v=value(w)≥1, j=v−1. Entry has n=2,v=3,j=2.

If v<2^n−1, a zero digit exists; the Y→X→Y increment gives v+1,j+1, preserving the invariant and width. If v=2^n−1, the word is all a, so overflow gives Y(b^(n+1),0,100 Q^(2^n−2)1).

In this 100 mode maintain length n+1, value v=j, i=2^n−2−v, with 0≤v≤2^n−2. Such v is strictly smaller than 2^(n+1)−1, so there is a zero digit. Each Y→X→Y increment increases v,j by one; for i>0, i decreases by one. At i=0 the endpoint instead has tail 11, v=j=2^n−1, length n+1. The word is exactly a^n b and j≥1. Its 11-mode Y→X→Y increment gives v=2^n, length n+1, j=2^n−1 and tail 101, restoring the first invariant at larger width.

This constructs an infinite chain of positive, fully defined primitive runs from the genuine blank entry. Every compatible completion agrees on every queried slot; hence every completion is nonhalting and satisfies any weighted bound.
