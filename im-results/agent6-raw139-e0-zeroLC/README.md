## E0 parent decomposition

The first query is D0 at step `query_step`. Its immediate-halt (`none`) branch is handled directly by `first_hole_weighted_halt`. The remaining **24 move branches** are partitioned into:

- **11 direct cycle children**;
- **12 broad D0 branches**, whose search has 13,323 initially unclassified leaves, 29 exact hard-witness exceptions, and two late multi-hole expansions;
- **one accepted D0=0RC five-hole Cover** at weighted bound 775.

The broad search's longest ordinary query is 80,371 steps, so the proposed parent weighted bound is **80,377** after the five blank-delay moves and halt tick. The hard-witness recipes and exact tables are in [`hard-witnesses.md`](hard-witnesses.md). The 13,323 leaves are computationally classified by the search/recipe layer; they are not a kernel-checked parent result until the generated Lean closure passes.

## Reusable proof generation

`emit_family.py` and `emit_chunks.py` now route every FAR witness type used by the hard-witness table:

- `block` and `phase`: existing block-FAR certificates;
- `mirror` and `mirrorphase`: block-FAR certificates for the reflected machine, transported back with `FARBi.reflectCompatible` and `FARBi.reflectNever` from the proved tape-reflection symmetry;
- `offset`: multi-block mask certificates generated by `generate_multi.py` and `emit_blockmask.py`, including the width-four specialization.

This removes the previous emitter gap for mirror and offset witnesses. The generated wrapper states the exact compatibility transfer from the original partial table; the Lean kernel must validate each transfer theorem in the target run.
