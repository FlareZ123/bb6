## Method and reproducibility

The 261 distinct saved automata from agent7-raw49/bulk_far_seeds.txt and rest_far_seeds.txt directly cover 81. Replacing one DFA transition destination (leaving the blank initial transition fixed) yields 223 cumulative; repeatedly mutating only selected successful witnesses produces 294,329,332,339 and no additional hits on the sixth pass. Duplicate a DFA state and redirect a nonempty subset of incoming edges (at most 3 redirected or at most 2 retained), with at most one altered outgoing edge from the duplicate; from seeds and winners of size <10 this yields 7 additional certificates. Single mutations of those yield 2 more. Applying state duplication to 10-state seeds/winners, allowing 11 states and using 128-bit saturation instead of 64-bit, yields 2 more. This is discovery; only the checked certificate proves nonhalting.
