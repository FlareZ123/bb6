## Whole-problem status and missing proof links (Critic review, 2026-11-02)

**BB(6) remains undetermined.** The results catalogued below establish source-connected, all-time **necessary reachability exclusions** for selected Machine C and P1 families; they do **not** classify either original blank-start machine, settle the other studied holdouts, provide a universal six-state upper bound, or establish a new exact BB(6) value. The number of excluded congruence classes should not be interpreted as a numerical measure of proximity to closure: the surviving infinite families may contain the decisive cases.

Two distinct verification tasks remain in addition to the local theorems:

1. **Rooted holdout decisions.** Obtain a halting witness with a certified raw step count or a genuine all-time nonhalting proof for each *specific blank-start root*. Transition-table isomorphisms alone do not transfer halting between different initial states. The [pinned 2026-10-16 portfolio ledger](../im-results/holdout-family-coverage/README.md) groups 663 historic rooted representatives into 435 unrooted transition tables, but intentionally establishes no halting-equivalence across distinct roots. Its one reviewed external nonhalting root is prior work, not a new result here.
2. **Exhaustive completeness and maximality.** Independently certify the reduction from *every* standard six-state, two-symbol machine to an exhaustive collection of proven halting/nonhalting representatives, including the machines eliminated before the dated holdout snapshot. For all halting representatives establish certified runtime bounds and exhibit a machine attaining the maximum. The current result index and [shared decision-gates synthesis](../im-results/2026-11-02-cross-holdout-decision-gates-agent19.md) do not supply this universal coverage proof.

For technical work on the remaining P1, B, C, Sheep, and D-root quantifiers, use the [cross-holdout decision-gates synthesis](../im-results/2026-11-02-cross-holdout-decision-gates-agent19.md). For source provenance and prior art, see the [dated source map](../resources/2026-11-02-bb6-research-map.md). Future additions should prioritize a genuinely reusable proof mechanism or a resolved root, with the novelty claim checked against external formalizations. Preserve intermediate certificates and computational sieves in `im-results/`.
