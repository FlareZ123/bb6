# BB(6): decision-oriented research board

**Review: 26 November 2026.** BB(6) is **not solved**. The externally reported six-state lower bound is `S(6) > 2 ↑↑↑ 5`; the repository has a verified exact primitive-transition score for the published champion, but no proof-carrying universal upper bound. The strongest accepted weighted-TNF conditional interface is the source-audited **68-residual Canonical68 E1 parent reduction** ([result and exactness audit](../results/2026-11-26-canonical68-E1-parent-reduction-agent6.md), combined source audit [run 45725315357](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/45725315357)). It is a conditional roster reduction: the 68 retained Covers at the champion score and the exact champion witness remain required. This board is a **navigation and missing-quantifier map**, not a timestamped progress log, a leaderboard of residue densities, or an assignment system.

For theorem hypotheses, exact source bridges, transitive axiom profiles, CI revisions, false starts, and current narrow subproblems, use the linked **primary dossiers**. ## Deciding a machine means closing its actual blank orbit

A valid six-state BB(6) completion requires: a source-faithful halting machine with a precisely specified primitive-step halting convention and exact step count, **and** an exhaustive partition of all remaining six-state, two-symbol blank-start tables into nonhalting proofs or verified smaller halting-time bounds. Partial classifications, finite simulations, a prepared-tape halt, or an infinite but incomplete arithmetic exclusion are not whole-machine decisions.
