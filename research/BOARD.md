This discharges the downstream composition, not those premises. The remaining mathematical bridge is a **universally quantified original-physical-machine count for the full `Inc0` and any earlier reset/prefix families**, followed by the genuine blank-root itinerary to the terminal endpoint. This is a complementary proof programme to authentic-root nonhalting, and can be attacked without expanding the astronomical numeral or rerunning finite samples. The generic [terminal-prefix/score adapter](../im-results/agent42-champion-terminal/TerminalAfterPrefix.lean) still supplies the final +6 root/+1 query correction for any proved TM63 prefix; no unconditional champion witness or exact global score has been established.

2. **Make the first-event partition exhaustive.** Record every applicable state/tape section, F/1 or other halt event, no-event branch, exceptional exit and lost tape information. Original-source table equality, raw first-event semantics and theorem axiom audits matter for that claim; repeated unrelated CI does not.
