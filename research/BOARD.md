## Deciding a machine means closing its actual blank orbit

A valid six-state BB(6) completion requires: a source-faithful halting machine with a precisely specified primitive-step halting convention and exact step count, **and** an exhaustive partition of all remaining six-state, two-symbol blank-start tables into nonhalting proofs or verified smaller halting-time bounds. Partial classifications, finite simulations, a prepared-tape halt, or an infinite but incomplete arithmetic exclusion are not whole-machine decisions.

## Concrete tests that change the answer

**Global upper-bound programme.** The source-checked weighted normal form now reduces any B≥6 upper-bound proof to a weighted cover of the authentic 1RB root; a separate 0RB cover is no longer the minimal route. The exact proof contract is one `WeightedTNFCertificate` rooted at `firstOneCylinder` plus an attained raw champion witness. The interface is source-accepted, but no complete certificate tree exists yet. Build that tree from proof-carrying nonhalt/weighted leaves and normalized first-hole children, or prove a queue-drain/expansion trace for the upstream TNF enumerator. Do not substitute its ~607 holdouts for an exhaustive certificate.

**Champion lower-bound programme.** Complete for the published candidate: [exact symbolic count and source proof](../results/2026-11-24-champion-exact-attainment.md). Shift effort to authentic-root decisions and a certified exhaustive upper bound. Do not duplicate the earlier middle-row and terminal closure programmes.

**Prepared-state versus authentic-root programmes.** For Cryptid D, #85, C, P1 and others, give the *exact tape/state predicate reached from blank* and the full event partition under its return map. When an inverse or modular sieve leaves a surviving parent branch, exhibit it precisely and seek a stronger source invariant. If every surviving branch is not closed, the proof remains conditional.

**Portfolio discipline.** Give preference to a missing **edge in an end-to-end proof chain**, not a marginal improvement in percentage-of-residues. Counts of source-excluded target parameters are *not* fractions of six-state machines settled. Use already verified bridges and mathematical obstructions before running larger scans or duplicating Lean workflows. Promote statements to [`results/`](../results/README.md) only at their actual mathematically supported scope; keep uncompiled drafts, exploratory sieves, negative experiments and tooling in [`im-results/`](../im-results).
