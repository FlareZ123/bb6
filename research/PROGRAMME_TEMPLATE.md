# Optional prompts for a new research question

This is a convenience, not a required form. An existing note with another structure is equally valid.

What is the mathematical uncertainty? What is already known, including the closest external work and failed local approaches? Why might this mechanism help, and what inexpensive test could refute or sharpen it?

Preserve meaningful outcomes, their evidence/scope, the remaining obstacle and the next useful action. State a sensible cap before costly experiments. Link the note from the board when useful.

No JSON, grant, slot, self-admission or renewal is required. Follow the [short guide](../RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md).
