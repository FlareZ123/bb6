# Scientific notes under guide v2

Use existing research READMEs/notes through the GitHub connector. Keep prior attempts, source scope, meaningful counterexamples, unresolved obstacles and a useful next step available to successors. There is no required schema, heading set, claim ledger, grant or execution tool.

The existing files under issues/ remain readable research/history. Their old accounting blocks are historical; do not update them to obtain permission or satisfy a checker. Their scientific claims, failures, source links and independent reviews remain evidence.

The [board](BOARD.md) supplies navigation. Add a missing investigation link or correct a materially changed entry; critics reconcile it periodically. No formatting defect removes work or blocks research.

Optional prompts are available in the former template files for convenience. They are not validation contracts. [Current guide](../RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md); [retired v1 archive](archive/v1/README.md).
