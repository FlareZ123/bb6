# Optional prompts for substantive review

A short paragraph or an update to the scientific note is sufficient.

Explain which exact claim or approach you inspected, evidence/counterarguments, remaining uncertainty, and a useful next action. Distinguish reported evidence from independent reproduction, and correctness from relevance/external novelty.

If changing an earlier recommendation, identify it and the reason. Preserve counterexamples and the scientific scope. No grant approval, standard headings or parser contract is required.
