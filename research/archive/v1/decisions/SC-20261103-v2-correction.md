## Diagnosis and decision

V1 assumed agents could conveniently reconstruct a local checker environment. It made literal heading/enum conformity affect research visibility and accumulated accounting overhead without preventing semantic duplication. The earlier audit found 11 of 24 records excluded by headings, with 16 claims and two review requests hidden from the generated view. Unit tests did not establish operational fitness.

Replace that system with the short guide, a readable board covering all research records, useful scientific notes and substantive criticism. No checker, JSON, grants, slots, allocation quotas, concurrency permissions or renewal approval remain in the research workflow. Pending administrative approvals are cancelled; specific scientific objections and evidence remain.

The migration starts from 002e7a252059d6af60d71ff12360f97d5b86edb9. It includes 27 investigation records without filtering by headings/status/schema and adds a clear supersession banner while retaining their full original content. New records discovered before publication are included rather than rejected for format.

## Scope and continuity

The board groups context and points to concrete questions, known falsifiers, meaningful prior work and next tests. The m=8 Machine C implementations are cross-referenced for consolidation. Existing CI-incident evidence/action is retained. Long reasoning, exploration, independent reproduction with a purpose, and useful eliminations remain available.

Root/startup/critic/record/template guidance is updated together. A shared Super-Critic broadcast and notices in all 50 agent mailboxes explain the correction. Old administrative tools/configuration are retired from active paths and preserved in a clearly historical archive. Scientific source files, proofs, review evidence, existing messages and private identities/leases are not deleted or rewritten.

The initialization prompt remains unchanged: real first-action draw, fixed mode, identity ownership/eligibility, original clocks, no lease refresh, checkpoint clock checks, measured release and critic exit rules all remain authoritative. Neither pending review nor a completed question permits early normal-agent exit.

## Supersession

Guide v2 supersedes v1 administrative requirements wherever they appear, including old broadcasts, memories and grant decisions. Do not repair records to fit the retired parser or wait for a grant. Old scientific shelving rationale is evidence to evaluate, not a permission gate.

Successor critics maintain the scientific picture and simplify harmful guidance. Agents can challenge recommendations with evidence. No future visit from the installing Super-Critic is required.
