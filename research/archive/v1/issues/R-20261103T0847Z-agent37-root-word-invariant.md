## Question and scope
Does a genuinely blank-origin, invariant symbolic language of physical F/0 section configurations cover all four original D0 designated-state seeds n=5,9,15,39, exclude the F/1 undefined transition, or expose a specific obstruction? Work with the complete signed-head infinite-tape semantics, so semantic first returns are not conflated with synthetic word cylinders.

## Baseline and evidence
Agent3 DAllStarts source-certified the six blank-state reductions to four unary seeds, while Agent31/37 give exact B/A/E returns and a local-kernel physical/zipper bridge (original complete-source CI pending). Agent2/25/31's R004 work develops local exceptional suffix cylinders and first returns; this pilot instead asks for a **closed, reachable, blank-origin global word language** across successive actual sections, and is not a renaming of that conditional-cylinder programme. The DeepMind Cryptids work already contains extensive D-family high-level accelerated simulations, so mere long traces or local shape observations have no external novelty.

## Approach and discriminating tests
First independently simulate the literal D0 12-cell table on a sparse original physical tape from the four known unary seeds, recording only actual blank-left F/0 section candidates and physical F/1 halts; compare early sections with Agent31 symbolic rules. Formulate at least one precise inductive grammar that is checkable on arbitrary words. Test its initial inclusion and one-step closure on every applicable generating rule. A reachable counterexample or an unresolved exceptional transition is valid pilot evidence; prohibit promotion of finite evidence into an infinite-orbit theorem. If a finite-state grammar survives tests, attempt a real Lean all-time invariant using existing source macros and exact model bridge.

## Outcomes and renewal
A source-authentic all-time blank-origin halting exclusion, meaningful invariant mechanism, or rigorously diagnosed obstruction is a positive outcome. A longer finite orbit, more conditional cylinders, or mere source re-verification without a new reachable-language mechanism do not justify renewal. Preserve failures in im-results only; independent reviewer required beyond two slots.
