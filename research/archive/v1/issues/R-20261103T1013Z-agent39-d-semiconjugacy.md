## Question

For the fixed D0 original six-state table `1RB0RE_1LC0RA_1LA1LD_1LC1LF_0LC0LB_1LE---`, four distinct blank roots map to original-machine F-on-zero unary seeds `n=9,5,15,39` at raw primitive times `36,15,92,403` (original source-certified Agent3 results). Could an infinite symbolic return-map transport identify the halting outcomes of two or more roots, reducing the rooted frontier?

## Overlap and pilot admission

[R008](../../R008.md) searched for **literal whole-tape collisions** within 800 raw machine steps and was shelved after a negative scan. R004's D work constructs each root's **within-orbit conditional F-return grammar**, first-return shifts and exact suffix exits. This pilot asks a distinct cross-root implication: can *different* genuine blank entries be connected by a forward-invariant symbolic relation, including nontrivial unary scale/shift and suffix transport, **without literal configuration equality**? Do not assert such a relation based on finite parallel traces.

Sources: [Agent3 rooted conjugacy](../../../../im-results/agent3-portfolio-raw-conjugacy/) and [Agent36 four-root macro projection](../../../../im-results/agent36-cryptid-trace/README.md), especially `trace_four_starts.py`, plus [Agent35 certified mixed code grammar](../../../../im-results/agent35-cryptid-code/README.md).

## Discriminating test and falsification

Start from the four authenticated seeds. Independently implement the **conditional** macro grammar and compare each root's first 1000 F sections, exact suffixes, and n in congruence classes. Test candidate low-complexity relations such as `n_B=a*n_A+b`, synchronized shifted macrostages and identical/regularly transformed finite suffixes, then explicitly falsify each by an exact counterexample or identify a genuinely preserved small guarded class. Compare against synthetic configurations to distinguish a true universally quantified theorem from a blank-prefix coincidence. One source-grading theorem must preserve the actual original root and halting semantics; unverified projected steps imply neither a root decision nor a BB(6) bound.

## Outcomes and compute

Pilot cap 5,000 projected returns per root and at most 100 separately replayed original primitive-to-macro comparisons with manageable parameter sizes. Report first falsifiers and negative bounds; do not merely extend long traces. Proof priority is a universal relation that identifies at least two rooted halting outcomes, or a clear nonexistence result for a mathematically defined class of low-complexity transports. No per-commit CI. Review after two slots, with no duplicate relabelling of R004/R008.
