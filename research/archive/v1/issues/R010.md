## Question and scope
Can the first-even-return map E of the actual six-state Sheep Machine admit a uniform forward inductive invariant, based on 3-adic residues and its value-dependent odd-block length, which excludes every power of two from its genuine entry 10? A partial theorem precisely restricting true blank-origin E-orbit states is valuable. The unresolved halting target is still a power of two; no such invariant is currently claimed.

## Baseline and evidence
Original table `1RB1LA_0LC0RC_1LE1RD_1RE1RC_1LF0LA_---1LE`; authentic blank returns `5→7→10`. Source-verified full equivalence `BusyBeaver.IsHalt target ↔ ∃a≥2, EvenReach 10 (2^a)`: `im-results/agent16-sheep-even-return/EvenSourceBridge.lean`, pinned successful CI 41505950547. The first-even return is `E(t)=1+3^h u` where `F(t)-1=2^h u`, u odd. Agent39 gives exact odd-block arithmetic, and Agent17/22/39 explain why bounded-depth inverse-tree exhaustion cannot close certain exponents. Agent14/40's even target modular exclusion programmes are reverse-image sieves. This pilot tests a distinct **forward reachable-language** mechanism; changing moduli or repeating existing inverse exclusions does not reset a grant.

## Approach and discriminating tests
First write an exact integer E simulator checked against literal F and the published semantic formulas. Track genuine orbit and a matched synthetic corpus, with residue states modulo 3^k and hidden 2-adic block length; ask whether any small residue/phase automaton gives a closed invariant missing all power residues. A concrete falsifier is a witnessed genuine orbit covering all power residues at small k or exhibiting transitions nondeterministic under the proposed finite-state summary. If this fails, test an inductive weighted valuation or regular word-language invariant that captures correlation beyond a fixed modulus. An exact all-parameter lemma and original-source bridge are required before any root-level claim.

Best outcome: source-connected forward invariant separating the genuine orbit from all 2^j. A meaningful weaker outcome: mathematically proved restriction of the reachable even states or a proved no-go theorem for a well-defined finite-state abstraction. Negative finite experiments or a failed candidate are intermediate and must be reported as such. Review the two-slot pilot on discriminating evidence, not on larger traces or increasing modulus alone.
