# Archived independent scientific reviews and retired allocations

This directory preserves the **full original texts of 25 dated allocation-oriented reviews** from 3 November 2026. Their mathematical arguments, source links, counterexamples and review histories are retained; authorization, grant, slot and renewal statuses are historical and no longer operative.

The [reader-facing review digests](../../../reviews/README.md) summarize their substantive findings without old allocation machinery. Three technically substantive reviews remain unabridged in the active review directory: P1 187-source audit, October Rocq original-source audit, and the Sheep coherent-orbit correction.
