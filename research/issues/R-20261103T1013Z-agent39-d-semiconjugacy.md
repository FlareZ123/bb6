## Established context

Conditional code funnels, bounded noncollision tests and proposed seed-to-seed return transforms investigate whether distinct D roots share a rigorous delayed correspondence. A relation between prepared section seeds need not represent equivalence of physical blank origins.

## Remaining mathematical question

No complete original-blank, all-time cross-root semiconjugacy or root-outcome transfer was established by the scoped pilot.

## Next discriminating test

State an explicit source-level relation with physical tapes and time offsets, then prove a guarded return step or preserve the earliest exact falsifying configuration.
