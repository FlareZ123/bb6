# Space Needle: source-authentic terminal inverse and root closure

**Status (7 November 2026): unresolved.** The Space Needle machine has neither a halting witness nor an all-time nonhalting proof. The exact BB(6) value remains unknown.

## Established source-level reduction

The original Cryptids high-level bridge identifies halting with the blank-start orbit from `N 1` reaching a counter whose shifted value is a power of two. Every one-step terminal feeder satisfies

`E(q,v,K): (2^(v+1)+3)q+3*2^v+v+3=2^K`.

The fifth-linear bound and its two exceptional root exclusions reduce the actual root question to a fifth-regular feeder list. The sixth-linear refinement then proves

`(q,v,K)=(214,3,12) OR (62600,5,22) OR 6v≤K+4`.

The two sixth-linear exceptional sources, 3446 and 4006494, are excluded from genuine blank-root reachability by exact reverse checks. The resulting source theorem is

`IsHalt target ↔ ∃ k q v, ReachN (oddTower q v) ∧ (v,q) ∈ NeedleSixthRegular.parents(k+1)`.

Membership in `parents(K)` is exactly the terminal equation plus `6v≤K+4`. The remaining exponent and reachability quantifiers are unbounded.
