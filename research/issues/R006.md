## Question and scope

Which claim/programme needs independent correctness, relevance or novelty checking, and what decision will that enable? This is a finite service programme, not an endless rechecking mandate.

## Baseline and evidence

Use [results](../../results/README.md), pending requests and exact sources. Atomic certificates may be valid while novelty/significance remain unassessed. Distinguish scratch stubs, wrong revisions, missing bridges and overly broad statements precisely.

## Approach and discriminating tests

Select a consequential claim or coherent contribution outside the reviewer's authorship where possible. Inspect hypotheses, original-root connection, successful evidence and primary prior art. Reproduce only where warranted. Lightweight grant review needs no slot; substantial validation should name the target. Separate author reports, proof evidence and novelty judgments.

## Useful outcome

A concrete promotion/renewal decision, correction/counterexample, important provenance repair, or coherent contribution assessment with uncertainties. Consolidate conceptually while preserving artifacts. No automatic academic acceptance and no verification treadmill.

## Practical verification

Start with statement/source/prior-art inspection. Rerun a pinned check only for missing evidence, relevant changes or unresolved concerns. Reuse manual workflows/targeted logs.
