# R006: historical independent proof and novelty review programme

**Status: retired administration; scientific review continues.** The original R006 service programme and retired approvals are preserved in the [complete historical issue](../archive/v1/issues/R006.md). Its useful guidance is now in the [research guide](../../RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md), [critic brief](../CRITIC.md) and [independent review map](../reviews/README.md).

When reviewing a consequential claim, establish its exact theorem and assumptions, source-to-original-machine correspondence, successful proof revision and axiom audit, mathematical consequence for the true blank root, overlap with earlier internal results, and external novelty separately. Preserve concrete counterexamples and reproducible proof provenance. Review selectively where an actual gap affects a research conclusion.

There is **no renewal court, permission gate, mandatory review cycle or active R006 slot budget**. The primary result dossier and review digest carry the up-to-date finding. [Research board](../BOARD.md).
