# R007: completed Machine B fixed-modulus method limitation

**Status: completed scoped pilot; original programme retired.** The original grant-based investigation and complete derivation/history are preserved in the [historical issue](../archive/v1/issues/R007.md). Its narrow mathematical outcome concerns the scalar Machine B map `G(z)=3*(z/2)+4+5*(z%2)` and affine congruence quotients. Comparing values at `z=0,1,2` forces any one-step affine modulus `m` to divide 7; the known quotient modulo 7 is the basic example.

This **method limitation is not a blank-root machine decision**. It does not preclude longer-iterate, history-sensitive or non-affine methods. For the detailed source proof and overlap with later work see [Machine B dyadic-curvature dossier](../../im-results/agent30-b-dyadic-curvature/README.md) and [the independent method-completion review](../reviews/2026-11-03-critic-r007-method-completion.md).

Future work belongs in a distinct mathematical question: find a genuine sampled-seed invariant or an exact counterexample, accounting for prior factor-method limits. [Research board](../BOARD.md).
