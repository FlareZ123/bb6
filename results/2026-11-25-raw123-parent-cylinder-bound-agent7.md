# Complete weighted bound for the raw123 six-hole cylinder

**Theorem.** Let (p) be the partial six-state, two-symbol table

`1RB---_1RC---_0RD---_1LD1RE_---0RF_------`.

For every (B\ge 10000), the original-machine weighted TNF semantics satisfy
`Cover (WeightedBound B) p`. In particular, every halting completion is bounded by 10000 primitive steps including its halt query and any compatible incoming blank-prefix delay as accounted for by WeightedBound. There are (25^6=244140625) compatible raw tables; the theorem is a cylinder bound, not a universal BB(6) upper bound or a claimed exact cylinder maximum.

The kernel-checked theorem is `BB6.Agent7Raw123.raw123_cover` in [Raw123ResidualClosure.lean](../im-results/agent7-raw123/Raw123ResidualClosure.lean). The exhaustive tree has 306928 cycle leaves, 68320 last-hole leaves, 11544 original open leaves and 16817 split nodes including the first F1 query. Of the open leaves, 11127 are covered by finite automata, 368 by inductive growth, nine by late last-hole checks, 19 by n-gram invariants and 21 by block-FAR invariants. The finite cut retains precisely the last 40; the final matching theorem checks exact table identity for every retained entry and applies its original-machine nonhalting witness.

The final width-seven block-FAR leaf required a sparse-support checker that derives the old full-row validity condition from the exact stored nonzero supports. Its soundness is proved in [ActiveSparseFAR.lean](../im-results/agent13-block-far/ActiveSparseFAR.lean), and its radix-packed data are generated by [emit_active.py](../im-results/agent13-block-far/emit_active.py). The final witness has 2876 left states and 4906 right nodes, with only 7826 active control rows.
