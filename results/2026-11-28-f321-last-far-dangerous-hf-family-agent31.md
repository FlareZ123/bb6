## Exact last-Far law

For a formal inverse H/F word \`w\`, [\`TerminalWordCandidate.lean\`](../im-results/agent31-f321-bulk/TerminalWordCandidate.lean) defines a positive integer \`terminalDenom w\` and proves
\[
u(w)+q(w)=2^{s(w)+1},\qquad q(w)\equiv1\pmod6,
\]
together with the exact terminal equation
\[
q(w)d=v(w)+2^{s(w)}(S(w)+c-1).
\]
Consequently a fixed word and fixed small-second anchor class encode at most one terminal parameter.

The stronger [\`TerminalWordLastFar.lean\`](../im-results/agent31-f321-bulk/TerminalWordLastFar.lean) shows that the earlier prefix is irrelevant to the 2-adic depth generated by the final Far. If the complete final word is \(p\,F\,H^b\) for an arbitrary earlier H/F prefix \(p\), then every genuine terminal-seeded formal inverse path satisfies
\[
\boxed{d+3=2^b z,\qquad z\equiv b\pmod2.}
\]
Thus the trailing High block after the **last Far** controls the \(-3\) 2-adic neighbourhood independently of all earlier inverse letters. In particular, odd \(b\) gives the exact valuation \(v_2(d+3)=b\), while even \(b\) forces at least one additional factor of two.

## An infinite source-faithful \(H^nF\) terminal family

[\`HighFarTerminalFamily.lean\`](../im-results/agent31-f321-bulk/HighFarTerminalFamily.lean) proves a uniform High-run construction and defines
\[
N_m=3m+4,\qquad T_0=37,\qquad T_{m+1}=8T_m-3.
\]
For every \(m\ge0\),
\[
7T_m=2^{N_m+4}+3,
\]
and with \(R_m=2T_m-(N_m+6)\) there is an exact source-counter run
\[
(R_m,1)\xrightarrow{H^{N_m}F}(T_m+1,T_m).
\]
The subfamily \(m=8k+6\), \(N_m=24k+22\), satisfies
\[
T_m\equiv37\pmod{384},\qquad R_m\equiv22\pmod{24},
\]
so it survives the accepted coarse terminal mod-384 gate and coarse root-anchor residue condition.
