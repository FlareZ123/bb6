With B1 fixed to `0RC`, **18 of the 25 possible C1 actions** have source-certified child results: five complete weighted Covers and thirteen nonhalting children. The full Raw44 parent remains open.

The canonical seven-hole parent is `1RB---_1RC---_0RD---_1LD1LB_------_------`. Fixing B1=`0RC` leaves six unassigned entries and therefore `25^6 = 244,140,625` complete machines. ## Source and proof boundary

The complete assembly runs use the existing `.github/workflows/agent38-m62-kernel.yml` family dispatcher. It reconstructs the pinned original Lean source closure with `tools/lean_compile_closure.py`, compiles the generated `zipCheck`/`certifiedZip` tree and all imported growth theorems, then runs `im-results/agent4-raw109/audit_axioms.py`. The axiom gate allows only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`, and rejects `sorryAx` and `native_decide`. All five assembly runs listed above succeeded.

The JavaScript discovery and generation scripts are not trusted proof oracles. Their finite trace trees, translated-cycle checks, n-gram certificates and symbolic recurrence lemmas are checked by Lean against the original machine definitions. A successful child Cover proves only its exact child domain.

**No full Raw44 seven-hole parent Cover is established.** The other B1 branches and the seven remaining C1 choices under B1=0RC are still obligations. These results do not yet remove Raw44 from the accepted Canonical38 residual roster, do not change the global residual count, and do not determine BB(6).
