## Proof mechanism

Untrusted discovery transfers a finite-stack periodic rewriting grammar from a known example to a different literal transition table. The resulting two-sided stack automaton and finite reachable-control relation are quotient-minimized and re-saturated. The unchanged Lean `FastBiDFA` checker then establishes an exact inductive invariant and the nonhalting theorem. The search is not in the trusted proof chain. The resulting Lean certificates depend on only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`; the CI check explicitly rejects `sorryAx` and `native_decide`.

Pre-minimization candidate states/edges were 3001/1059, 9670/2136, 2953/1077, 12644/1739, 4917/1571, and 9882/2041, in table order. All six quotients were checked as original Lean source.
