# BB(6): mathematical result map and open proof obligations

**Research synthesis, 2026-11-23:** The exact six-state, two-symbol Busy Beaver step value is **unknown**. This repository has not established a complete blank-start decision for the remaining holdouts, a global six-state upper bound, or a complete maximum-attaining certificate. A source-verified *necessary gateway exclusion* for one machine is not a decision of that machine.

## External BB(6) lower-bound baseline (not a repository theorem)

BusyBeaverWiki currently identifies `1RB1RA_1RC1RZ_1LD0RF_1RA0LE_0LD1RC_1RA0RE` as the BB(6) champion, discovered by mxdys on 25 June 2025, and reports the published step lower bound `S(6) > 2 ↑↑↑ 5`. Its machine page gives an approximate halting-time analysis between `2 ↑↑↑ 5` and `2 ↑↑↑ 6`, plus a separate, more precise sigma-score estimate `10 ↑↑ 10 ↑↑ 10 ↑↑ 8.10237`. The sigma score is not the primitive step count. The wiki links a Rocq analysis at [busycoq/SOBCv5.v](https://github.com/ccz181078/busycoq/blob/3f302b87f5fb933c46e97672ffbb6907f373fb6e/verify/SOBCv5.v#L10210-L11283).

This is external published evidence, not a repository theorem. The repository's [Agent 48 champion-root bridge](../im-results/agent48-champion-root/README.md) formalizes a conditional machine equivalence; it does not import the unconditional external halting proof or prove the exact primitive step count. A separately [source-certified exact TM63 terminal-section cost](../im-results/agent42-champion-terminal/README.md), [CI 42739779283](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/42739779283), now proves for every `L,a` the literal source's terminal `m0` macro uses exactly `59+12*(L+a)` successful transitions before the final undefined halt query. It allows arbitrary tape exterior, and does **not** certify the other still-conjectural counted macros, the entire champion trajectory, or BB(6). The source links and notation distinctions are recorded in [the external baseline note](../resources/BB6-current-frontier-2026-11-06.md).

## New original-source Machine C exclusion (23 November 2026)

A [combined mathematically certified source theorem](2026-11-23-machine-c-low-559-source-exclusion-agent13.md) excludes **559 of 4096 disjoint residue classes** of the necessary Machine C low-stop family \(m=512z+67\), where the genuine low stopping target is \((1,2^{2m+3}-(3m+4))\). The thirteen disjoint congruence clauses are \(z\equiv13\pmod{16}\), \(5\pmod{64}\), \(25\pmod{32}\), \(9,21\pmod{128}\), \(65\pmod{512}\), \(1729\pmod{2048}\), \(3521\pmod{4096}\), \(165\pmod{256}\), \(105\pmod{512}\), and \(469,869,585\pmod{1024}\). Every excluded infinite family has a complete original-section inverse-parent contradiction, rather than merely a finite quotient or an unbounded numerical search.

The [original sixteen-class two-parent proof](2026-11-23-machine-c-low-source-sixteen-agent13.md) and [original three-parent valuation-six proof](2026-11-23-machine-c-low-d4-three-parent-agent13.md) supplied the previously accepted 65/512 baseline. Seven new cylinder proofs now extend this to 559/4096; the original-source Lean 4.36.0-rc2 proof checks passed for the two new three-parent families in [CI 45252580437](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/45252580437) and [45253172542](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/45253172542), and for five additional two-parent families in [45254908517](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/45254908517).

The exact corresponding **lossless Machine C blank-halting equivalence**, [`blank_halts_iff_six_low559`](../im-results/agent13-residual-frontier/LowM67All559Criterion.lean), is accepted by [CI 45255502605](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/45255502605): 253 original-source dependency modules, axiom profile \[`propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`\], no `sorryAx`. This only excludes **13.65% of this one necessary low-stop parameter family**; the remaining low and high halting branches are unbounded, and **Machine C and exact BB(6) remain unsolved**. The mathematical proof, explicit forced inverse paths, disjointness count, code, and limitations are presented in the linked combined result rather than duplicating every atomic experiment here.

## Historical result notes and publication status

The original eighteen standalone gateway notes comprise three Machine C component results and fifteen P1 entries. The separately derived 8 November source-indegree/distribution theorem is a structural counting result, rather than an additional gateway exclusion. The Machine C components are integrated by the synthesis above. Nine Machine C reports were moved, with their full proofs and provenance preserved, to the [publication qualification archive](../im-results/machine-c-publication-audit/README.md) because they are quotient-known or specializations of broader source theorems. **The inclusion of any component note is not an independent claim of external priority.** An academically publishable contribution requires theorem-level comparison with the strongest repository and external work, a coherent synthesis, and traceable verification.

The former approximately 19-kB, certificate-by-certificate index is preserved in the [historical catalogue](../im-results/legacy-results-index-20261105-critic.md), whose links now point to the retained results or the qualification archive. Larger results should be presented through synthesis documents that state the new method, existing baselines, exact remaining quantifier, and source chain.

**Research aim:** Prove a complete authentic blank-root halt/nonhalt decision, or a proof-carrying exhaustive six-state maximality certificate. Parameter counts, longer finite traces, synthetic predecessor counts and disconnected necessary conditions provide research tools; none alone measures a fraction of BB(6) solved.
