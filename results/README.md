## Cryptid D: total affine reduction and executable equivalence

The [global affine completeness theorem](2026-11-24-d-global-affine-completeness-agent32.md) reports original-source Lean 4.36.0-rc2 acceptance, with standard axioms only. For every finite suffix and residue, a guarded section has either a uniform return from `3k+d` to `8k+c` with exact quadratic live cost `12k²+bk+t`, or a uniform halt-gate path, with positive time. The well-founded executable adapter eliminates the previous fixed-fuel limitation. Its `blank_total_iff` reduces authentic D0 blank halting to existence of a terminal accelerated plan from I9. The proof is not a finite scan.

The [prepared halting DAG](../im-results/agent32-d-route-dag/README.md) reports a source-certified family of 1,057,916,215,296 disjoint prepared unary halting residues modulo 3^108; it does not establish reachability from blank. The earlier [contextual ternary suffix theorem](2026-11-23-cryptid-D-certified-ternary-suffix.md) and [external cylinder adapter](../im-results/agent32-cryptid-padding/ExternalTM62-D0-Bridge.md) are reusable components.

The [density-one theorem](2026-11-24-d-density-one-agent3.md) proves that for every fixed finite suffix the nonhalting prepared unary inputs have density zero, in fact count `O_v(N^(1−epsilon))` for an absolute positive epsilon. Its finite max-plus certificate checks all 1024 long canonical prefix classes and gives a five-return rank-sum bound of −118 over 243 residue words. This is a computer-assisted mathematical proof with exact Python arithmetic and previously kernel-certified source reductions, not an end-to-end Lean theorem. The same certificate gives [universal phase separation](2026-11-24-d-four-root-phase-separation-agent3.md) of the four selected macro orbits, including exceptional suffixes.

The [conditional non-coalescence theorem for four authentic Cryptid-D root paths](2026-11-24-four-root-conditional-noncoalescence.md) strengthens this to the original-source section-return system: assuming the four blank starts do not halt, every finite return depth exists and distinct root/depth pairs have different unary section lengths. The integrated Lean 4.36.0-rc2 audit has no `sorryAx`. This rules out all-time coalescence but does not decide any root's halting status.

The [sharp quadratic first-exit and global primitive-clock law](2026-11-24-d-primitive-clock-law-agent2.md) uses a locally kernel-accepted original-source rank-defect amortization to bound every right-half service by 315(2R+8)(R+3), with a matching quadratic-order lower family. Its paper composition yields total clock T=(12/55)N²+O(tN+t²+1) for t selected events, with N the final live unary length or terminal marker distance, and disjoint exact all-depth clock bands for the four authentic roots. It constrains possible terminal times without deciding whether they exist.

**Missing:** determine actual terminal reachability or prove all-time avoidance. The [exact Boundary-to-Portfolio bridge](../im-results/agent3-d-boundary-portfolio/README.md) is now locally kernel-accepted and identifies four distinct unary seeds I9, I5, I15, I39 with respective live-transition entry offsets 36, 15, 92, 403, and the exact live-count versus final-query convention. Individual root decisions still require arithmetic terminal reachability or all-time avoidance; density one cannot decide a fixed integer.

## Cryptid #693: exact gate duality and obstruction to finite algebraic safety invariants

[Semilinear obstruction theorem](2026-11-24-693-semilinear-obstruction-agent3.md): in the exact two-counter acceleration, every interior cone of every congruence cylinder contains arbitrarily large halting prepared states, and no infinite orbit stays in finitely many affine lines. Consequently no nonempty Presburger-definable terminal-free forward-closed set exists in these coordinates. The arithmetic conjugacy, mass growth, odd-branch closed form and rational-cycle obstruction are locally Lean-checked; the congruence/cone synthesis is a mathematical paper proof, with the upstream source import not yet compiled. The companion [inverse-ray sieve](../im-results/agent3-693-inverse-rays/README.md) gives sublinear terminal-parameter counts from reset seeds. The [parity-separated inverse and dual-semigroup equivalence](../im-results/agent3-693-dual/README.md) gives a total decision procedure for an individual backward target and an exact affine-line membership problem for the next gate. The [aperiodicity and polynomial-congruence obstruction](2026-11-24-693-polynomial-obstruction-agent3.md) proves that every infinite trajectory has infinitely many accumulation directions and rules out finite Boolean polynomial-sign/fixed-modulus safety invariants, strictly extending the semilinear theorem. These last two global obstruction claims are paper proofs; the arithmetic reduction is locally Lean-checked. The authentic root remains open.

## Machine C: exact stopping criterion and inverse-image structure

The [joint source stopping criterion](../im-results/agent19-c-odd-dyadic/JointCriterionIntegration.md) isolates low and high stopping branches. The strongest currently reported dedicated low-ray criterion excludes 1551 of 4096 classes of the necessary family `m=512z+67`; [source theorem](../im-results/agent13-residual-frontier/LowM67All1551Criterion.lean), [CI 45275168303](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/45275168303), [maintained proof dossier](../im-results/agent13-residual-frontier/LowRayFullFan.md). The preceding 559 and 1327 class counts are superseded milestones, not additional disjoint coverage.

The newer [modular high-stop inverse cone](2026-11-24-machine-c-modular-high-stop-inverse-cone.md) and [low-stop counterpart](2026-11-24-machine-c-modular-low-stop-inverse-cone.md) give sound recursive parent exclusions; the [unified modular-cone dossier](../im-results/agent42-modular-cone/README.md) records exact-height and arbitrary-depth direct-block refinements accepted in [CI 45366985296](https://github.com/FlareZ123/bb6/actions/runs/45366985296). These are refinements of the exact blank-halting criterion. Do not assume that independent sieve counts are additive or that every separately accepted criterion has already been combined.

The [density-zero inverse-reachability theorem](2026-11-24-machine-c-shadow-density-zero-agent21.md) establishes uniform sparse dyadic residue covers for the entire reachable section lattice and both full stopping families. A compatible-word collision and uniform amplification yield finite-tree extinction; the direct large-right bridge then transfers this to actual reachable targets. The abstract and complete-parent-law-conditional Lean modules compile locally with standard axioms only; the thin original-source import adapter remains uncompiled. This proves a qualitative asymptotic restriction, not emptiness of the residual set or a fixed-root decision.

The structural mathematical results include [exact section indegree distribution](2026-11-08-machine-c-exact-indegree-distribution-agent34.md), [two-return image density](2026-11-08-machine-c-exact-two-step-image-density-agent34.md), [strict section-image hierarchy at all depths](2026-11-09-machine-c-all-depth-strict-section-image-hierarchy-agent4.md), and [gateway synthesis](2026-11-07-machine-c-gateway-exclusion-synthesis.md). They distinguish certified source reachability exclusions from paper-level lattice counts.

[Aperiodicity of complete section itineraries](2026-11-24-machine-c-aperiodic-section-itinerary-agent12.md): every infinite section orbit has a non-eventually-periodic complete branch sequence. Exact affine growth forces a binary repetend whose only possible primitive mixed shift word is (2,1,1); a parity-aware two-block calculation eliminates it modulo 17. The affine-growth lemma and finite modular checks are Lean-accepted locally, with the full number-theoretic classification proved in writing. This rules out fixed repeating affine-branch-word growth proofs, not nonperiodic orbits.

[Prepared halting rays in every congruence cone and the semilinear obstruction](2026-11-24-machine-c-semilinear-obstruction-agent12.md): exact ordinary affine maps of shifts 1,2,3 act transitively on the projected finite modular space, by commutators yielding translations by 48 and 80. A primitive Diophantine equation lifts this steering into an infinite prepared halting ray in every residue class and open slope interval. Combined with the finite affine-line obstruction, this rules out every nonempty semilinear (Presburger-definable) terminal-free forward-closed invariant in the section counters. Written proofs; commutator identities locally Lean-accepted, constructive ray generator with coefficientwise guard checks. The authentic blank orbit remains undecided.

[Polynomial concentration and polynomial-congruence obstruction](2026-11-24-machine-c-polynomial-congruence-obstruction-agent12.md): an infinite section orbit cannot be eventually confined within O(M^-epsilon) of any finite set of ratios, for any fixed epsilon>0. Positive limiting directions must lead to near-zero visits, whose high shifts and odd affine coefficients force injective mass recovery from a short word and small endpoint. The resulting polylogarithmic word count contradicts the orbit's mass-crossing frequency. Combining this with the prepared halting-ray theorem excludes every nonempty terminal-free forward-closed invariant given by a finite Boolean combination of polynomial sign and fixed-modulus congruence tests. The modular uniqueness lemma is locally Lean-checked; asymptotic arguments are paper proofs.

- [Haar-null ancestry and uniform dyadic density completeness](2026-11-25-machine-c-dyadic-density-completeness-agent12.md): arbitrary-multiplicity coincident affine word cylinders force the critical ordinary ancestry set to have Haar measure zero. A uniform fiber and affine-line argument handles all exceptional births in finite congruence closures, proving high-stopping residue survival tends to zero uniformly in depth and low-stopping survival tends to zero. Algebra and generic sieve soundness kernel-checked; the measure argument is a written proof. No pointwise exclusion follows.

**Missing:** close the entire unbounded low and high parameter existentials for the authentic blank orbit, or exhibit a genuine terminal witness. Residue and lattice densities are not measures of six-state machines decided.
